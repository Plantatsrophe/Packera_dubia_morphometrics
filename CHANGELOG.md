# Changelog

All notable methodological, statistical, figure, and SQLite relational schema changes to the North American *Packera* phenotyping and species delimitation pipeline are documented in this file. Versioning tracks the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill Herbarium (`NCU`) Standard Operating Procedure (`UNC-BOT-SOP-2026-04-REV5`) and the integer SQLite `PRAGMA user_version` (`PACKERA_SCHEMA_USER_VERSION`) enforced in `scripts/core/db_schema.py` and `scripts/core/db_utils.R`.

The structure follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [5.0.0] - 2026-11-11 (`UNC-BOT-SOP-2026-04-REV5` | SQLite Schema `20261121`)

### Added
* **Canonical 12-Plate 300-DPI PNG Figure Suite (`scripts/vision/step02_figures.py` and `scripts/figures/`):** Built a modular 12-plate visualization suite (`fig02a` through `fig06b`) rendered exclusively as 300-DPI PNG rasters via `ragg::agg_png` (`0` PDF figure outputs). All plates scale dynamically from $N = 1$ intraspecific morphotype cohorts up to $60+$ *Packera* species and infraspecific ranks (`subsp.`, `var.`, `f.`) through a combinatorial 12-hue Tol/Okabe-Ito colorblind-safe palette paired with 5 unfilled hollow marker geometries, ICN rank-aware italicization, automatic 1D `Can1` raincloud switching when $G = 2$, and equal-area `EPSG:5070` (`NAD83 / Conus Albers`) maps.
* **Native `LeafMachine3` Visual Asset Integration (`lm3_assets/`, `ect_overlays/`, `lm3_sheet_overlays/`):** Wired Step 02 (`scripts/vision/step02_figures.py`) to export unlimited taxon-indexed leaf cutouts (`render_leaf_products`) and polar radial ECT overlays (`render_radial_ect_overlay`) across basal and cauline tracks alongside capped (`max_sheets = 24`) whole-sheet diagnostic overlays, reused directly in `fig02a`, `fig03b`, and `Triage_Dashboard_Interactive.html`.
* **Unified Step 06 Species-Complex Delimitation (`scripts/analysis/taxon_complex_discovery_utils.R`):** Integrated unsupervised Leiden/Louvain modularity and silhouette-optimal metric Ward.D2 species-complex discovery directly into Step 06, complete with Taxon $\times$ GMM Morphotype `[K#]` subclustering when $N_{\text{taxa}} \le 2$, isolated RNG seed restoration (`.with_preserved_rng_seed`), SQLite `taxon_complexes` persistence, and twin-free 23-column `empirical_taxon_complexes.csv` exports.
* **Self-Contained Offline Curatorial HTML Dashboard (`Triage_Dashboard_Interactive.html`):** Added a zero-dependency interactive browser workbench in Step 06 featuring OOF Cohen's $\kappa$ reliability banners, empirical complex chips, 300-DPI PNG plate links, instant taxon/priority/subtype filtering, and filtered 17-column CSV export (`triage_queue_filtered.csv`).
* **Repository Reference Documentation (`LICENSE`, `CITATION.cff`, `docs/DATA_DICTIONARY.md`, `docs/ENVIRONMENTAL_DATA_SOURCES.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`):** Added complete relational schema contracts, environmental raster provenance and divisor tables, CFF 1.2.0 citation metadata, MIT + AGPL-3.0 boundary licensing, and contributor QA gates.
* **Four-Stage Focal Species-Complex Orchestrator (`resolve_packera_dubia_complex.sh`, `scripts/pipeline/resolve_focal_species_complex.py`):** Added an automated runner that sequences Stage 1 regional complex discovery, Stage 2 focal-plus-neighbor consensus triage, Stage 3 purified intraspecific morphotype and ecotype evaluation, and Stage 4 spatially thinned Gold anchor stability verification, compiling cross-stage CSV and Markdown taxonomic adjudication summaries from SQLite and run reports.
* **Persistent Warm-R Audit Runner and Pruned `222`-Test Suite (`scripts/tests/_r_session.py`, `scripts/tests/run_audit.py`):** Consolidated `Rscript` test execution onto persistent per-stage warm R worker sessions (`step03` through `step06`), pruned 30 obsolete and duplicate tests, merged the Step 06 synthesis and complex-discovery test modules, and added `--fast` (`--python-only`) to `run_audit.py`.

### Changed
* **SQLite Schema `20261121` and `v_integrative_triage_master` View Precedence (`scripts/core/db_schema.py`, `scripts/core/db_utils.R`):** Promoted `hopkins_adjusted_doy` in `v_integrative_triage_master` to `COALESCE(s.hopkins_adjusted_doy, t.hopkins_adjusted_doy)` so refreshed Step 05 circular Hopkins flowering adjustments immediately supersede older `curation_triage` values, and synchronized missing Step 04 OSR (`min_congener_mahal_d2`, `min_congener_taxon`, `open_set_novelty_p`), `scale_source`, `is_passive`, `AR_inv_std`, `reproductive_trait_status`, and `ecoregion_can1..2` columns across Python and R.
* **End-to-End Multi-Plant and Focal-Taxon Handoffs (`02_segment_and_extract.py`, `05_multimodal_spatial_rf.R`, `06_triage_dashboard_synthesis.R`):** Preserved composite `(catalogNumber, plant_individual_id)` identities across un-georeferenced and passive individuals in Step 05, persisted full reference cohorts to SQLite in Step 06 prior to `--focal-taxon` queue filtering, and wired `purge_segments_and_runs()` cleanly to `--purge-segments`.
* **Read-Only `LeafMachine3` Step 02 Adapter and R-Side Step 03 QC (`03_fourier_extractor.R`):** Persisted 100% of segmented leaves, 120-vertex tip-up `lamina_base`-anchored contours, and $32 \times 360$ polar ECT `.npy` arrays to Tier A storage in Step 02, delegating multi-leaf clump and frame-margin filtering to `evaluate_step03_lm3_efa_qc()` in R prior to `Momocs::efourier(..., norm = FALSE, start = FALSE)`.

## [4.2.0] - 2026-09-18 (SQLite Schema `20260918`)

### Added
* **Automated Environmental Layer Provisioner (`scripts/data_prep/download_environmental_layers.py`):** Added resumable, atomic pre-Step-05 provisioning for 20 USDA-NRCS SOLUS100 GeoTIFFs, pre-joined USGS SGMC CONUS bedrock GeoPackages (`sgmc_conus_lithology.gpkg`), and cohort-targeted $1^\circ \times 1^\circ$ 3DEP $30\text{ m}$ DEM tiles.
* **Stratified Authority Pool Cap and Population-Cohesion Lineage Gate (`authority_weighting_utils.R`, `06_triage_dashboard_synthesis.R`):** Capped `Tier_2_Silver` weight at $\le 40\%$ of active taxon anchor pools whenever $N_{\text{type+gold}} \ge 5$, enforced type-specimen FastMCD immunity, and added the four-part `cryptic_cluster_cohesion_pass` gate ($N \ge 5$, $\ge 3$ gatherings across $\ge 2$ $10\text{ km}$ demes, multi-herbarium representation, and non-taphonomic symmetry) before calling `Putative_Cryptic_Species`.
* **Open-Set Recognition (`OSR`) and Confident Learning (`04_gmm_morphotools.R`):** Added minimum squared Mahalanobis distance screening across all active congeneric centroids (`min_congener_mahal_d2`, `open_set_novelty_p`) alongside Northcutt Confident Learning (`is_confident_label_noise`) to prevent unsampled outgroups from mimicking novel cryptic taxa.

## [4.0.0] - 2026-06-02 (Two-Tier SQLite WAL & `LeafMachine3` ONNX Migration)

### Added
* **Two-Tier Transactional SQLite WAL Architecture (`data/packera_pipeline.sqlite`):** Separated invariant Tier A specimen, organ, and environmental caches (`data/`) from run-scoped Tier B analytical cohorts (`runs/<RUN_ID>/` and `runs/latest`) with composite `(run_id, catalogNumber, plant_individual_id)` keys and virtual R*Tree spatial indexing (`environmental_rtree`).
* **Isolated `LeafMachine3` ONNX Vision Stack (`setup_leafmachine3.sh`, `scripts/vision/`):** Adopted `uv`-locked `LeafMachine3` ONNX Runtime inference (`yolo26x_det_1280`, `UNet++ v9` specimen cleanup, ruler tick-lattice calibration, `yolo26x_seg_1024` 3-class Leaf/Petiole/Hole segmentation, 31-keypoint pose, and $32 \times 360$ polar ECT extraction) alongside optical capitulum phenophase staging (`capitulum_phenology_classifier.py`).
* **10 km Deme Plasticity Mitigation, `RRPP` Allometry, and $0\text{ to }15\text{ cm}$ Rhizosphere Modeling:** Added within-deme phenophase residualization and $Q_{ST}$ whitening (`plasticity_mitigation_utils.R`), ruler-calibrated `RRPP` multivariate allometry, Isometric Log-Ratio (`ILR`) soil texture coordinates, circular Hopkins flowering phenology (`step05_phenology_ecotypes.R`), and authority-weighted MaxEnt + Spatial Block-CV Random Forest ensemble niche models (`step05_sdm_maxent.R`).
