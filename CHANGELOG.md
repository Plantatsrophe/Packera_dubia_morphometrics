# Changelog

All notable methodological, statistical, figure, and SQLite relational schema changes to **Plantmatics** (the North American *Packera* phenotyping and species delimitation pipeline) are documented in this file. Versioning tracks the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill Herbarium (`NCU`) Standard Operating Procedure (`UNC-BOT-SOP-2026-04-REV5`) and the integer SQLite `PRAGMA user_version` (`PLANTMATICS_SCHEMA_USER_VERSION`, aliased as `PACKERA_SCHEMA_USER_VERSION`) enforced in `scripts/core/db_schema.py` and `scripts/core/db_utils.R`.

The structure follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [5.5.0] - 2026-11-14 (`UNC-BOT-SOP-2026-04-REV5` | SQLite Schema `20261126`)

### Added
* **Public Cytology Harvester and Label Scanner (`scripts/data_prep/download_public_cytology.py`):** I built a modular cytology ingestion utility (`< 450` lines) that fetches Chromosome Counts Database (`CCDB`) records paired with National Botanical Data Center `ICNA` zip archives (with path-traversal validation) alongside *Flora of North America* Vol. 20 accounts and regex-guarded herbarium specimen label scans (`extract_label_cytology_mentions()`). `resolve_packera_cytology_taxon()` drops non-target Old World *Senecio* records (`Senecio vulgaris`) while standardizing *Packera* basionyms (*Senecio tomentosus* into *Packera dubia*), and it writes flat `data/cytology/public_packera_cytology_records.csv` and `data/cytology/public_packera_taxon_cytology_summary.csv` outputs plus Tier A `cytological_records` paired with `taxon_cytology_summary` alongside an offline SQLite sync mode.
* **SQLite Schema `20261126` Cytology Tables and Master View Columns (`scripts/core/db_schema.py`, `scripts/core/db_utils.R`):** I incremented `PLANTMATICS_SCHEMA_USER_VERSION` (aliased as `PACKERA_SCHEMA_USER_VERSION`) from `20261125` to `20261126`, added Tier A `cytological_records` paired with `taxon_cytology_summary`, and added six columns (`cytology_linkage_tier`, `voucher_sporophytic_2n`, `voucher_ploidy_level`, `taxon_reported_cytotypes`, `is_multi_cytotype_taxon`, plus `cytotype_reproductive_barrier_pass`) to both `curation_triage` and `v_integrative_triage_master`.
* **Step 07 Non-Circular Cytology Integration (`scripts/analysis/step07_cytology_integration.R`):** I added `run_step07_cytology_integration()` (`< 380` lines) to link `C1_Direct_Cytovoucher` specimens alongside `C2_Exsiccatae_Cytoduplicate` sheets and high-confidence `Step06` `nQuire` calls (`|delta_logl| >= 4.0`). The module evaluates multi-anchor postzygotic heteroploid barriers (`cytotype_reproductive_barrier_pass`) while executing a strictly post-hoc Gigas audit written to `cytotype_concordance_summary.csv` and keeping baseline outputs invariant under zero cytology data.
* **Cytology Integration Test Suite (`scripts/tests/test_public_cytology_integration.py`):** I added `< 380`-line QA tests covering meiotic and aneuploid record parsing plus approximate counts, basionym resolution, label regex precision, zip-slip protection, offline cached sync, Step 06 monomorphic `2x` Empirical Bayes priors, Step 07 heteroploid decision rules, cross-cytotype propagation vetoes, plus zero-cytology invariance.

### Changed
* **Empirical Bayes Ploidy Priors in Step 06 (`scripts/data_prep/06_ingest_genetic_data.py`):** I added `_load_db_cytology_priors()` so `_infer_sample_cytotype()` and `_compute_eb_polyrad_dosage()` use empirical `modal_ploidy_level` from `taxon_cytology_summary` for low-SNP monomorphic diploid samples (`ploidy = 2` instead of a generic `4x` fallback) while keeping well-supported `nQuire` `4x`/`6x` likelihood calls (`delta_logl >= 4.0`) intact.
* **Cytotype Barrier Routing and Cross-Cytotype Deme Guards (`scripts/analysis/07_triage_dashboard_synthesis.R`, `scripts/analysis/step07_genomic_propagation.R`, `scripts/figures/step07_figures.R`):** I updated `apply_taxonomic_decision_matrix()` so morphologically divergent (`D^2 > qchisq(0.95)`) cohesive heteroploid clusters (`cytotype_reproductive_barrier_pass == 1`) elevate to `Putative_Cryptic_Species` tagged `[Postzygotic Heteroploid Barrier: ...]` even when flowering windows overlap, whereas morphologically overlapping heteroploid clusters route to `Subspecies_Ecotype` tagged `[Intraspecific Cytotype Series: FCM/squash verification recommended]`. Across `propagate_conditional_deme_priors()` and `propagate_cluster_gdi_governance()`, mixed-ploidy demes block cross-cytotype (`2x` vs. `4x`) transfer while `step07_figures.R` adds ploidy legend annotations.

## [5.3.0] - 2026-11-14 (`UNC-BOT-SOP-2026-04-REV5` | SQLite Schema `20261125`)

### Added
* **Four-Tier Step 07 Genomic Propagation (`scripts/analysis/step07_genomic_propagation.R`):** Added `run_step07_genomic_propagation()` to project validated Step 06 genomic signals onto unsequenced herbarium sheets under strict uncertainty guards. `propagate_exsiccatae_isoduplicates()` assigns `G1_Exsiccatae_Isoduplicate` to matching `exsiccatae_key` duplicates while vetoing nomenclatural types and divergent mixed mounts (`D^2 > qchisq(0.99, p)`). `propagate_conditional_deme_priors()` assigns `G2_Conditional_Deme_Hull` (`geno_posterior_prob` clamped to `[0.65, 0.90]`) only when a specimen lies within `2.0 km` geodesic distance (`coordinateUncertaintyInMeters <= 1000`, or `<= 1.0 km` when missing) on matching `is_azonal_lithology`; the specimen must also fall inside the 95% `Basal_Rosette` Mahalanobis plus `18.0`-day circular Hopkins hull and clear sympatric alternatives by `(min(alt_d2) - d2_cand) >= 4.0`.
* **`Tier_0` Anchor Calibration and Cluster `gdi` Governance (`scripts/analysis/step07_genomic_propagation.R`, `scripts/analysis/genomic_triage_utils.R`):** Added `resolve_tier0_anchor_overrides()` to weight sequenced `Tier_0` anchors at `4.0` and flag DNA-contradicted or admixed sheets (`evict_from_given_anchor`) for exclusion from taxon centroids and circular Hopkins means. `fit_tier0_calibrated_projection()` fits a Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage discriminant model (`lambda = 0.20`) with LOPO-CV temperature scaling on `[1.0, 5.0]` and assigns `"Sensu_Lato_DNA_Required"` (`tier0_abstention_flag = 1L`) under weak confidence or inter-lineage overlap. `propagate_cluster_gdi_governance()` copies validated cluster `coalescent_gdi` (`cluster_propagated_gdi`) onto non-admixed cohort members and leaves abstained records unpropagated, while `build_genomic_markdown_summary()` formats propagation tallies inside `Packera_Taxonomic_Revision_Summary.md`.
* **SQLite Schema `20261125` Propagation Columns (`scripts/core/db_schema.py`, `scripts/core/db_utils.R`):** Incremented `PLANTMATICS_SCHEMA_USER_VERSION` to `20261125` and added six columns to `curation_triage` (`genomic_propagation_tier`, `tier0_calibrated_taxon`, `tier0_calibrated_prob`, `tier0_abstention_flag`, `deme_mahal_d2`, `cluster_propagated_gdi`), all exposed in `v_integrative_triage_master`.
* **Exsiccatae Duplicate Ranking and Step 07 Consensus Wiring (`scripts/data_prep/06_ingest_genetic_data.py`, `scripts/analysis/07_triage_dashboard_synthesis.R`):** Updated `_link_sample_to_voucher()` to prefer imaged primary duplicates (`is_primary_duplicate = 1`) when `exsiccatae_key` or `recordNumber` matches multiple herbarium sheets. Updated `compute_probabilistic_consensus_scores()` to scale `G1_Exsiccatae_Isoduplicate` at `0.95` alongside `G2_Conditional_Deme_Hull` at `0.75`, and to apply `t0_soft` (`0.50 * lopo_cv_kappa`) only when `geno_link_scale == 0.0` and `tier0_calibrated_prob >= 0.80` without abstention; `apply_taxonomic_decision_matrix()` enforces cluster `gdi` oversplit blocks (`[Cluster Coalescent Oversplit Block: propagated gdi=...]`) alongside corroborated candidate notices.

### Changed
* **Schema Migration and Contract Test Coverage (`scripts/tests/`):** Updated all active `PLANTMATICS_SCHEMA_USER_VERSION` assertions to `20261125` and added `step07_genomic_propagation.R` alongside `genomic_triage_utils.R` to the 500-line module check in `test_code_hygiene.py`. Expanded `test_genomic_integration.py` with Python exsiccatae-linking tests plus a batched warm-R suite that exercises Tier 1 exsiccatae propagation with mixed-mount vetoes, Tier 2 deme hulls with sympatry guards, `Tier_0` eviction or abstention, and cluster `gdi` governance alongside zero-genomics invariance; added a companion warm-R test in `test_triage_dashboard_synthesis.py` that verifies `t0_soft` consensus priors alongside `Tier_0` phenology dominance and zero-genomics decision invariance.
