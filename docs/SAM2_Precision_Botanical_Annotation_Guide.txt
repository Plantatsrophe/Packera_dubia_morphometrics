1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & PROBLEM RESOLUTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In dried herbarium vouchers of Packera dubia, basal leaf petioles terminate directly 
at the caudex (root crown), physically merging with fibrous roots and rhizomes. 
Because both structures consist of dark, pressed, fibrous plant material without high-contrast 
color boundaries, naive single-point prompts cause SAM 2 (Segment Anything Model 2) to 
bleed across the caudex and lump roots, stems, and petioles into a single mask.


This guide provides the complete protocol for using the upgraded precision SAM 2 annotator 
(`scripts/annotation_and_training/annotate_with_sam2.py`), which incorporates six botanical constraint, 
pre-labeling, and inspection mechanisms:
  a. Automated LM2 + SAM 2 Pre-Labeling (--prelabel-with-lm2): Pre-populates basal leaf masks on each sheet using Stage 1 LeafPriority.pt + Stage 2 PointRend + SAM 2 refinement for rapid manual tweaking.
  b. Bounding Box Prompts (Shift + Drag): Restricts SAM 2 attention strictly within the targeted lamina/petiole.
  c. Negative Exclusion Points (Right-Click): Explicitly forces roots, overlapping blades, or background out of the mask.
  d. Multimask Proposal Cycling (Tab): Cycles through SAM 2's 3 hierarchy levels (sub-lobe vs. blade vs. clump).
  e. Two-Click Knife Slicing ('k'): Performs clean, straight dissections across the petiole-caudex junction on both candidate and pre-labeled masks.
  f. Morphological Margin Tuning ('+' / '-'): Provides 1-pixel binary dilation/erosion to dial in fuzzy tomentose margins.


Following this protocol, 50-100 vouchers can be cleanly annotated in under 2 hours to provide 
a gold-standard native-DPI single-leaf crop dataset (`data/annotations/lm2_leaf_crops/`) for fine-tuning 
LeafMachine2's Stage 2 Detectron2 PointRend Leaf Segmenter (`leaf_seg_rcnn_v2_1`).

3. BOTANICAL CLASS ONTOLOGY (STAGE 2 BASAL LEAF SCHEMA)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To align directly with LeafMachine2's Stage 2 PointRend Leaf Segmenter (`leaf_seg_rcnn_v2_1`), 
interactive HUD commits focus on the two active basal leaf classes (while retaining full 7-class 
ontology compatibility for legacy masks):


Class ID | Class Name           | Active HUD | Botanical Definition & Diagnostic Landmarks
---------|----------------------|------------|-----------------------------------------------------------
   0     | basal_leaf_whole     | YES (Key 0)| Intact/unoccluded basal leaf lamina (crenate/serrate/lyrate)
   1     | basal_leaf_partial   | YES (Key 1)| Partially occluded or folded basal leaf with intact half-blade
   2     | cauline_leaf         | Legacy     | Sessile, lyrately-pinnatifid leaves on the flowering stalk
   3     | cauline_stem         | Legacy     | Main vertical flowering stalk / scape / peduncle
   4     | root_rhizome         | Legacy     | Subterranean dark fibrous roots, rhizomes, and rootlets
   5     | basal_rosette_clump  | Legacy     | Dense, overlapping basal rosette center / crown
   6     | capitulum            | Legacy     | Inflorescence head / cylindrical involucre / phyllaries

Navigation & Viewport Controls:

  - Middle-Click Drag:   Smooth viewport pan across high-resolution herbarium scans.
  - Space + Left-Drag:   Alternative smooth viewport pan.
  - 'f':                 Fit to screen (resets zoom to 1.0x and centers sheet).
  - Click '[< Prev (b)]' / 'b' / Left Arrow: Navigate to previous voucher sheet WITHOUT saving or modifying annotations.
  - Click '[Next > (n)]' / 'n' / Right Arrow: Navigate to next voucher sheet WITHOUT saving or modifying annotations.

Segmentation & Prompting:
  - Left-Click:          Add positive foreground prompt point (Green landmark).
  - Right-Click:         Add negative exclusion prompt point (Red landmark).
  - Ctrl + Z / 'z' / Bksp / NumPad . / Del: Undo last prompt point (removes single inclusion or exclusion point and re-runs SAM 2 in real time; also clickable via the top-right '[Undo Pt (Ctrl+Z)]' HUD button).
  - Shift + Left-Drag:   Draw bounding box constraint (restricts SAM 2 search space).
  - Tab / NumPad /:      Cycle through the 3 SAM 2 multimask candidate granularities (sub-lobe vs. blade vs. clump).
  - 'k':                 Two-click knife tool for petiole/caudex slicing (Click 1: start cut, Click 2: end cut; works on active candidate or pre-labeled saved instances).
  - '+' / '=' / NumPad +: Single-pixel binary dilation (expands mask margin outward into arachnoid tomentum).
  - '-' / '_' / NumPad -: Single-pixel binary erosion (contracts mask margin inward from background glare/bleed).
  - 'p':                 Toggle polygon lasso mode (Left-Click to trace custom vertices, Enter/Right-Click to finalize).

Inspection & Visibility:
  - 'o' / NumPad *:      Toggle view mode: semi-transparent mask fill vs. 1-pixel boundary contour line (for crenation inspection).
  - Hold 'v':            Hold-to-peek: Temporarily hides all mask and prompt overlays to reveal bare specimen pixels.
  - '[' and ']':         Decrease / increase mask overlay alpha transparency (steps of 0.1, clamped between 0.10 and 0.90).
  - Pre-populated Masks: Existing or --prelabel-with-lm2 annotations automatically render with distinct colors and counts when moving between sheets.

Commit & Session Management:
  - Keys '0'–'1' / NumPad '0'–'1': Instant basal leaf class assignment and instance commit (0: basal_leaf_whole, 1: basal_leaf_partial; works with NumLock ON or OFF).
  - 'u':                 Undo last committed instance on current sheet.
  - 'c':                 Clear current candidate points, bounding box, knife line, and candidate masks.
  - Click '[Save (s)]' / 's' / Enter / NumPad Enter: Save current sheet annotations (fast sub-10ms save to YOLO txt and PNG masks).
  - 'q' or Esc:          Save modified sheet annotations and quit application cleanly (syncs COCO JSON on exit).
  - Window 'X' Button:   Safely close window (saves if dirty, syncs COCO JSON on exit).




5. BOTANICAL BOUNDARY BEST PRACTICES FOR DENSE PACKERA ROSETTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The basal rosettes of Packera dubia present extreme morphometric challenges: petiole bases 
densely crowd at the caudex, blades overlap at acute angles, margins possess subtle 
crenate-dentate teeth, and petioles/sinuses often bear flocculent white tomentum (pubescence). 
Adhere to the following operational protocols to ensure research-grade segmentation:

A. Disentangling Clumps via Multimask Proposal Cycling (Tab):
   - When placing a positive point on a basal blade that overlaps another, SAM 2 frequently generates 
     an ambiguous response. By default, the top-confidence mask may capture the entire multi-leaf clump.
   - Do NOT immediately add numerous negative points. Instead, press `Tab` to cycle through the 3 
     internal proposal granularities computed by SAM 2:
       * Granularity 1 (Sub-part): Single leaf lobe or crenation tooth.
       * Granularity 2 (Part): The isolated individual leaf blade (lamina).
       * Granularity 3 (Whole): The aggregated rosette clump.
   - In 85% of crowded cases, cycling with `Tab` reveals the clean, isolated blade mask instantly.

B. Validating Tooth and Sinus Margins via Contour-Only Mode ('o'):
   - Standard solid mask overlays can obscure fine crenate/dentate margin architecture and secondary teeth.
   - Press `o` to switch from filled mask rendering to a crisp 1-pixel boundary contour line.
   - Inspect the contour against the raw leaf margin at 4.0x–8.0x zoom:
       * Verify that tooth apices and sinus depths are faithfully traced without bridging adjacent teeth.
       * Hold `v` to peek the bare image and check whether apparent margin irregularities are true teeth 
         or pressed petiole fragments of overlapping leaves.
   - Press `o` again to return to filled overlay mode before committing.

C. Petiole-Lamina Separation via the Two-Click Knife Tool ('k'):
   - Where the petiole inserts into the caudex or where an elongate petiole stalk attaches to the blade base,
     press `k` to activate Knife Mode.
   - Click once at one edge of the junction, then click across the opposite edge.
   - The annotator severs the mask along the cut line and automatically prunes away the disconnected 
     fragment, retaining only the target lamina segment (or slicing pre-labeled instances in place).

D. Managing Arachnoid Tomentum with Morphological Margin Tuning ('+' / '-'):
   - *Packera dubia* (formerly *P. tomentosa*) displays woolly, arachnoid pubescence (tomentum) along 
     the petiole and blade margin. Depending on lighting, SAM 2 may either under-segment by clinging 
     to the green/brown lamina core or over-segment into white woolly halos.
   - Press `+` to apply a single-pixel 3x3 binary dilation if the mask cuts inside the true laminar margin.
   - Press `-` to apply a single-pixel 3x3 binary erosion if the mask bleeds into diffuse edge hairs or background paper.




6. STEP-BY-STEP ANNOTATION PROTOCOL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For each herbarium voucher sheet:


Step A: Viewport Navigation & Basal Cluster Orientation
  - Hover the cursor over the basal rosette and roll the Mouse Wheel up to zoom in (2.0x to 8.0x).
  - Middle-Click Drag (or hold Space + Left Drag) to center the caudex and basal leaves in view.
  - Press 'f' at any time to reset zoom to 1.0x and survey the full voucher sheet.


Step B: Annotating Intact & Partial Basal Leaf Blades
  1. If launched with `--prelabel-with-lm2`, inspect the pre-populated basal leaf masks. Use 'k' (Two-Click Knife)
     to trim any residual petiole stalks or 'u' to remove any noisy instance.
  2. To annotate a new leaf, hold Shift + Left-Drag a bounding box around the target leaf blade, or Left-Click 
     directly in the mid-lamina.
  3. Press Tab to select the individual blade candidate proposal over the clump proposal.
  4. Press 'o' to inspect margin contours along the crenations. If hairs cause bleeding, press '-' once.
  5. If a petiole stalk bleeds into the caudex, press 'k' and click two points across the blade-petiole junction to sever it.
  6. Press '0' to instantly commit an unobstructed blade as `basal_leaf_whole` (Green), or press '1' to commit a 
     partially occluded/folded hemi-blade as `basal_leaf_partial` (Mint Green).


Step C: Save and Advance
  - Press 's' or Enter to save current sheet annotations (`data/raw_annotations/{id}.txt` and `data/raw_annotations/masks/{id}_*.png`).
  - Press 'n' (or Right Arrow) to advance to the next voucher sheet, or 'b' (or Left Arrow) to navigate to the previous voucher.
  - Press 'q' or Esc to save modified annotations, synchronize the cropped single-leaf COCO dataset (`data/annotations/packera_train_coco.json` and `data/annotations/lm2_leaf_crops/`), and exit cleanly.




7. DATASET BUILDING & STAGE 2 POINTREND FINE-TUNING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Once 50-100 vouchers are annotated in `data/raw_annotations/`, compile the Stage 2 
single-leaf cropped COCO dataset (`data/annotations/packera_train_coco.json`) and fine-tune 
LeafMachine2's Stage 2 Detectron2 PointRend Leaf Segmenter (`leaf_seg_rcnn_v2_1`):


# Step 1: Compile native-DPI 12%-padded single-leaf crops and COCO JSON (if not already synced on exit)

  --export-coco \
  --masks-dir data/raw_annotations/masks \
  --images-dir data/raw_vouchers \
  --crops-dir data/annotations/lm2_leaf_crops \
  --output-coco data/annotations/packera_train_coco.json \
  --val-split 0.15


# Step 2: Fine-tune LeafMachine2 Stage 2 PointRend Leaf Segmenter (leaf_seg_rcnn_v2_1)

  --coco-json data/annotations/packera_train_coco.json \
  --images-dir data/annotations/lm2_leaf_crops \
  --iterations 1000 \
  --output-weights models/lm2_packera_pcd_finetuned.pth
