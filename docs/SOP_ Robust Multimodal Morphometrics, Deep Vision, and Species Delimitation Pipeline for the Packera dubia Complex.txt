1. Executive Summary & Purpose
This SOP documents the migration from the custom-trained YOLOv8/SAM 2 prototype to the community-standardized LeafMachine2 (LM2) pipeline (Weaver et al. 2024). LM2 provides pre-trained Plant Component Detectors (PCD), automatic ruler scale isolation, physical millimeter conversion, and leaf ROI cropping; original custom scripts are preserved in scripts/_archive/. LM2 integrates with the 4-tiered basal leaf extraction and symmetry-reconstruction protocol, the 6-tiered herbarium misidentification mitigation architecture, Momocs EFA, mclust GMMs, MorphoTools2 passive CDA, DINOv2 self-supervised embeddings, Cleanlab label noise curation, and spatialRF/SoilGrids/WorldClim niche modeling.
2. Methodological Framework & Botanical Challenges
2.1 The Challenge of Overlapping Basal Leaves and Herbarium Non-Plant Artifacts
Botanical computer vision for Packera must address label & stamp bleed, missing petioles, and rosette clump fusion. LeafMachine2, utilizing the LeafPriority detector and PointRend sub-pixel boundary refinement, coupled with the 4-tiered extraction protocol (Tier 1 pristine silhouette, Tier 2 hemi-blade bilateral symmetry reflection, Tier 3 open Chebyshev polynomials, Tier 4 whole-rosette DINOv2 embeddings), overcomes these systematic bottlenecks.

Following extraction, the pipeline deploys a 4-Tiered Hierarchical Basal Leaf Extraction Protocol:

   * Tier 1 (Direct Pristine Extraction): Vouchers possessing at least one basal leaf with an Unoccluded Completeness Score (UCS≥0.85) and Solidity ≥0.72 are segmented directly into clean, closed silhouettes for 12-harmonic EFA.
SOP: Robust Multimodal Morphometrics, Deep Vision, and Species Delimitation Pipeline for the Packera dubia Complex
   * Tier 2 (Hemi-Blade Bilateral Symmetry Reflection): When basal leaves are partially occluded on one lateral margin or petiole, but possess a completely unobstructed half-blade (from apex to base along the midrib), the algorithm detects the primary midrib axis, isolates the unobstructed half-margin, and symmetrically reflects it across the midrib axis in OpenCV to synthesize a 100% complete, unoccluded bilateral silhouette for closed EFA.
SOP: Robust Multimodal Morphometrics, Deep Vision, and Species Delimitation Pipeline for the Packera dubia Complex
   * Tier 3 (Open-Outline Analysis & Landmark Morphometrics): For heavily crowded or damaged rosettes where neither half-blade is clean, open margin curvature is analyzed via orthogonal polynomials (Momocs::opoly) and sliding semi-landmarks in geomorph, coupled with scalar caliper traits (petiole length, max blade width, apex angle).
SOP: Robust Multimodal Morphometrics, Deep Vision, and Species Delimitation Pipeline for the Packera dubia Complex
   * Tier 4 (Dense-Rosette Deep Vision Embeddings): Concurrently, the unsegmented, full basal rosette patch (including leaf density, petiole crowding, and arachnoid tomentum texture) is passed directly to the DINOv2-ViT-B/14 vision transformer to extract holistic, segmentation-free 768-dimensional phenotypic embeddings.

2.2 Six-Tiered Herbarium Misidentification Mitigation Framework
The pipeline implements: (1) Authority Stratification (Gold/Silver/Bronze), (2) Label-Blind GMM Discovery, (3) Passive Sample CDA in MorphoTools2, (4) Multi-Modal Consensus (SoilGrids/WorldClim/phenology), (5) Confident Learning in Cleanlab, and (6) Digital Triage Queue.
2.3 LeafMachine2 Configuration & Botanical Extraction Safeguards
LM2 settings are optimized for high-throughput: PCD confidence threshold 0.72, batch_size 50, 8 workers, CUDA acceleration, PointRend instance segmentation, ruler scale detection, and multi-plant DBSCAN spatial clustering.
