## 1. Executive Summary and Theoretical Framework

I established this protocol to conduct automated high-throughput vegetative phenotyping and herbarium triage across North American ***Packera* Á. Löve & D. Löve** (Asteraceae: Senecioneae) with the taxonomically recalcitrant ***Packera dubia* complex** as my primary dissertation baseline. The lineage presents acute identification challenges on pressed sheets, as developmental heterophylly produces radical shape shifts between juvenile rosettes and mature cauline leaves, whereas manual curatorial annotations frequently confuse sympatric congeners.

I frame morphological diagnosability within the **Unified Species Concept (USC; de Queiroz 2007)** through **Integration by Congruence (Padial et al. 2010)**. Under the USC, species represent separately evolving metapopulation lineages. Lineage divergence generates morphological diagnosability and reproductive isolation alongside ecological divergence and reciprocal monophyly as contingent properties along the speciation continuum. I evaluate automated vegetative shape data alongside five independent lines of evolutionary and infrastructural evidence:

1. **Reproductive Macro-Morphology and Anthesis:** I quantify capitulum aspect ratios and phyllary counts alongside floret dimensions and 3-tier optical anthesis staging.
2. **Cytotaxonomic Cytotypes:** I integrate chromosome numbers and polyploid series ($2n = 44, 46, 88$; Kowal 1975) that separate diploid and tetraploid lineages.
3. **Reduced-Representation nextRAD Phylogenomics:** I align morphometric clusters against high-density SNP datasets that resolve reticulate evolution and hybrid swarms across lineage boundaries.
4. **0 to 15 cm Rhizosphere and USGS SGMC Bedrock Stack:** I cache edaphic profiles in SQLite `environmental_cache` and `environmental_rtree` through 0 to 15 cm depth-weighting (`(5 * v_0_5 + 10 * v_5_15) / 15`), where I combine SSURGO map units (`ssurgo_rock_outcrop_pct` and `ssurgo_bedrock_depth_cm`) and 30 m POLARIS grids alongside 100 m SOLUS100 layers, 6-class USGS SGMC lithology (`sgmc_lithology_class`), 10 m to 30 m USGS 3DEP topography, and Climatic ($D_{\text{clim}}$) versus Edaphic ($D_{\text{edaph}}$) niche equivalency tests (Warren et al. 2008).
5. **Central Relational WAL Architecture (`data/packera_pipeline.sqlite`):** All pipeline stages transact against a centralized SQLite WAL database housing Tier A shared tables (`taxon_synonyms` and `vouchers` alongside `plant_individuals`, `extracted_leaves`, `voucher_reproductive_phenology`, `environmental_cache` with `environmental_rtree`) and Tier B run-scoped tables (`pipeline_runs` and `run_active_cohort` alongside `leaf_efa_harmonics`, `otu_morphometrics`, `spatial_rf_predictions`, `curation_triage`, and `taxon_complexes`, with master view `v_integrative_triage_master`). Under schema version `20261121`, the database exposes dynamic Open-Set Recognition distances (`min_congener_mahal_d2`, `min_congener_taxon`, `open_set_novelty_p`), topological Euler Characteristic Transform descriptors (`ECT_PC1..PC3`, `Cauline_ECT_PC1..PC2`, `ect_lobe_complexity`, `cauline_ect_lobe_complexity`, `ect_bilateral_asym`, `heteroblasty_ect_distance`), Confident Learning noise flags (`is_confident_label_noise`), and lineage population cohesion indicators (`cryptic_cluster_cohesion_pass`).

This multi-tiered relational design separates immutable raw observations from run-specific statistical cohorts, so I can test alternative taxonomic hypotheses without risking data contamination.

## 2. Phase 1 Protocol: Ingestion, Standardization and US EPA Level III Ecoregions (`01_voucher_harvester.py`)

I designed Phase 1 to solve the foundational challenge of herbarium data intake: raw specimen databases contain corrupted taxonomic names and collector duplicate conflicts alongside geographic clustering that distorts statistical models when records enter uncritically.

* **Darwin Core Schema Contract and Relational UPSERT (`vouchers`):**
  I harvest occurrences into SQLite `vouchers` (PK: `catalogNumber`) via atomic UPSERT while I resolve synonyms through `taxon_synonyms`. I export a synchronized CSV `data/tables/curated_vouchers.csv` with 26 Darwin Core columns: `catalogNumber`, `institutionCode`, `scientificName`, `species_raw`, `recordedBy`, `recordNumber`, `identifiedBy`, `determiner_raw`, `determiner_tier`, `type_status`, `county`, `stateProvince`, `decimalLatitude`, `latitude`, `decimalLongitude`, `longitude`, `coordinateUncertainty`, `year`, `month`, `day`, `eventDate`, `regional_group`, `image_path` alongside `exsiccatae_key`, `is_primary_duplicate`, with `duplicate_count`, whereas duplicate conflict indicators (`exsiccatae_taxonomic_conflict` and `exsiccatae_gold_taxon`) persist directly in SQLite `vouchers`.
* **Dynamic Taxon Standardization and GBIF Synonym Expansion:**
  I standardize binomials and basionyms dynamically through `expand_taxon_synonyms_gbif()`. When I omit taxon flags, the harvester queries all *Packera* species across the genus through `discover_genus_packera_species_gbif()`. When I pass `taxon` or `focal_taxon` CLI flags, the harvester restricts analysis strictly to those specified taxa, and preflight reconciles any requested-taxon vouchers stored in SQLite `vouchers` into the active run cohort via `reconcile_curated_vouchers_csv_with_db()`.
* **Configurable Geographic Scope:**
  I support `geographic_scope {eastern_na,north_america,global}` (default: `eastern_na`), which enforces `decimalLongitude` bounds `[-106.65, -50.0]` during GBIF queries alongside a `typeStatus="*"` rescue query for historical types, whereas `north_america` and `global` retain western occurrences. When `exclude_western` is active, I exclude Western US states and Western or Northern Canadian provinces (`BC, AB, SK, YT, NT, NU`) and require $\ge 3$ georeferenced records east of $-104.05^\circ\text{W}$, governed by `countries` (default `["US", "CA"]`).
* **Pre-Download Deduplication and WebP Storage:**
  I deduplicate exsiccatae (`primary_duplicates_only=True`) before I download images, so the harvester retains primary sheets (`is_primary_duplicate == TRUE`) and unconditionally preserves verified nomenclatural types. Full-sheet imagery in `data/raw_vouchers/` and crops in `data/cropped_patches/` default to quality-92 `.webp` compression (`method=4` via PIL), which yields a 70% to 80% disk reduction relative to JPEG or PNG with zero edge degradation.
* **Official US EPA Level III Ecoregion Point-in-Polygon Delineation:**
  I assign `regional_group` strictly through spatial intersection against official **US EPA Level III Ecoregions** (Omernik & Griffith 2014; `data/environmental/epa_ecoregions_l3/`):
  $$\text{regional\_group}_i = \text{Ecoregion}_k \iff (x_i, y_i) \in \mathcal{P}_k$$
* **Ungeoreferenced Specimen Fallback Policy:**
  When coordinate data are missing ($y_i = \text{NA}$), I evaluate the US Census county boundary polygon $\mathcal{C}_i$:
  $$\text{regional\_group}_i = \begin{cases} \text{Ecoregion}_k & \text{if } \frac{\text{Area}(\mathcal{C}_i \cap \mathcal{P}_k)}{\text{Area}(\mathcal{C}_i)} \ge 0.95 \\ \text{NA} & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$
* **Nomenclatural Type Hierarchy and Priority Engine:**
  - `PRIMARY_TYPE_STATUSES = {"HOLOTYPE", "LECTOTYPE", "NEOTYPE", "EPITYPE", "ISOTYPE", "ISOLECTOTYPE", "ISONEOTYPE"}` (`Tier_0_Primary_Type`, rank 2).
  - `SECONDARY_TYPE_STATUSES = {"SYNTYPE", "ISOSYNTYPE", "PARATYPE", "ISOPARATYPE", "TYPE", "COTYPE"}` (`Tier_0_Secondary_Type`, rank 1).
  - I resolve fine-grained ICN priority through `classify_nomenclatural_type_subrank()` (`2.2` primary, `2.0` isotype, `1.5` syntype, `1.0` paratype; Turland et al. 2018). I exempt verified types from uncertainty or partial-date filters and retain them unconditionally during spatial thinning and outgroup selection.
* **3-Layer Dynamic Authority Engine (`parse_determiner_tier` and `classify_determiner_tier`):**
  - **Layer 1 (Monographer Heuristics and Determiner Isolation):** Dynamic authority discovery (`discover_corpus_authorities`) evaluates `identifiedBy` independently of `recordedBy`, and it falls back to `recordedBy` only when `identifiedBy` is blank or explicitly references the collector, which prevents collector-as-determiner false Gold promotions. I block non-person database adjustment strings (`"Nomenclatural Adjustment"` and `"Nomenclature update"` alongside `"Taxonomic update"` and `"Annotation"`). I require either a curated North American *Packera* and Senecioneae specialist entry (`PACKERA_SPECIALISTS` and `SPECIALIST_PATTERNS`: Barkley, Trock, Kowal, Weakley, Bain, Mahoney, Fuller, Freeman, Cronquist, Greenman, Weber, Löve, Spellenberg, Pippen, Uttal, Fernald, Rydberg, Heller, Kral, Godfrey, Sorrie, Nesom, Strother, Turner, Golden, Greene, Moore, Boufford, alongside Britton) or a protologue author token with explicit first-name or initial disambiguation, which blocks ambiguous bare surnames (Goodwin et al. 2015; Maldonado et al. 2015).
  - **Layer 2 (Protologue Author Extraction):** I parse basionym and combination authors directly from Darwin Core `scientificName` strings with `extract_protologue_authors()`, so sheets annotated by a nomenclatural author become `Tier_1_Gold`.
  - **Layer 3 (Corpus Determiners, Monographer Loan Protection and Batch-Transfer Demotion):** Determiners who identify $\ge 5$ specimens across $\ge 3$ herbaria become `Tier_1_Gold` (where type annotations carry $2\times$ weight and duplicate conflict must remain $\le 15\%$). In `apply_corpus_authority_tiers()`, I protect multi-herbarium and multi-species monographer loans by preserving `Tier_1_Gold` when a curated specialist annotates across multiple species or institutions even when `dateIdentified` is unrecorded. Conversely, to guard against uncritical folder-level synonymy stamps, I demote undated single-herbarium, single-species batch transfers ($\ge 25$ sheets across $\ge 10$ historical collectors with $\ge 85\%$ concentrated in one species at one institution where `recordedBy != identifiedBy`) from `Tier_1_Gold` to `Tier_2_Silver`, so they undergo out-of-sample Mahalanobis and Confident Learning screening. Personal field collections (`recordedBy == identifiedBy`) and explicitly dated specialist annotations remain `Tier_1_Gold` (Goodwin et al. 2015; Maldonado et al. 2015). Curated herbaria with $\ge 5$ sheets holding $\ge 1$ type become `Tier_2_Silver`, whereas unverified collections receive `Tier_3_Bronze`.
* **Preflight Authority Refresh and Congeneric Neighbor Filtering (`run_pipeline.sh`):**
  During preflight, the orchestrator re-applies `apply_corpus_authority_tiers()` and filters `auto_neighbors` candidates against `^Packera\s+[a-z]{3,}$`, so `outgroup_taxa` always retains valid congeneric binomials and excludes malformed family tokens.

Now that Phase 1 establishes an authoritative, georeferenced specimen cohort, I feed these vetted vouchers directly into the vision pipeline.

## 3. Phase 2 Protocol: Pure LeafMachine3 ONNX Pipeline and Botanical Gatekeeping (`02_segment_and_extract.py`)

Herbarium sheets present challenging visual artifacts: white mounting tape and fragment packets alongside discolored paper and overlapping stems frequently cause standard bounding-box detectors to fail. I address this challenge by deploying **LeafMachine3** (`lm3`) ONNX Runtime models alongside botanical gatekeeper algorithms.

### 3.1 LeafMachine3 Inference Stack (`scripts/vision/`)
* **Scale Calibration (Modules 1, 6 and 7):** Module 1 (`lm3_mp_conversion_factor__sqrt_fit`, $R^2 = 0.905$, MAE 2.3%) predicts resolution priors; Module 6 ensemble detects rulers via `lm3_ruler_classifier`; Module 7 (`lm3_ruler_segmenter` with Tick-Lattice Solver and FieldPrism markers) establishes physical scale (`pixels_per_mm`, `is_calibrated_mm`).
* **Stage 1 Plant Detection, Dissected PCD Settings and Asymmetric Nested-Box Deduplication (`lm3_plant_detector__yolo26x_det_1280`):** I ingest herbarium sheets into a `max_working_dim = 3200` working frame (`cv2.INTER_AREA`) and execute Stage 1 plant and organ detection at $1280 \times 1280$ resolution (`conf = 0.25`, `iou = 0.55`, `include_partial = true`) with dynamic rectangular stride-32 letterboxing (`ultra_rep.letterbox(auto=True, stride=32)`, `cv2.INTER_LINEAR_EXACT`) alongside float32 reciprocal scaling (`*= float32(1.0) / float32(255.0)`). Because deeply pinnatisect and bipinnatisect *Packera* blades (*P. glabella*, *P. aurea*, *P. paupercula*, *P. plattensis*, *P. tomentosa*) are frequently scored by Stage 1 as `Leaf_PARTIAL` (`class_id == 1`) or receive a high-confidence sub-box on a terminal lobe nested inside a lower-confidence parent whole-leaf bounding box, I retain both `Leaf_WHOLE` (`class_id == 0`) and `Leaf_PARTIAL` (`class_id == 1`) candidates (`segment_partial_leaves = true`) and apply parent-preserving asymmetric nested-box deduplication (`overlap = 0.95`). When a smaller leaf box is nested inside an enclosing parent leaf box ($\text{area}_{\text{parent}} \ge 1.35 \cdot \text{area}_{\text{child}}$ and $\text{intersection} / \text{area}_{\text{child}} \ge 0.90$), I retain the enclosing parent full-leaf bounding box and suppress the nested sub-lobe box.
* **Whole-Sheet Specimen Segmentation and CIE $L^*a^*b^*$ Paper Cleaning (`lm3_specimen_segmenter`):** Module 4 (`Specimen_Segmenter` `UNet++ v9`, $768 \times 768$) generates 4-class whole-sheet probability maps (`bg` and `plant_main` alongside `text_bars` and `paper`). I apply `paperclean_llr_hyst_keepblobs` (`LLR_Hyst_KeepBlobs`) log-likelihood hysteresis in CIE $L^*a^*b^*$ color space at `max_working_dim = 3200` strictly at the whole-sheet level to isolate the herbarium mounting sheet (`specimen_paper_cleaned_mask`) and plant foreground (`plant_binary_mask`), which removes mounting-paper pockets inside deep leaf sinuses while it preserves detached pinnae.
* **Stage 2 Leaf Instance Segmentation, Focal Instance Disentanglement, Rachis-Bridged Pinna Retention and Native `LeafMachine3` Delegation (`lm3_leaf_segmenter__yolo26x_seg_1024`):** I extract tight `crop_pad = 0.0` bounding-box crops from Stage 1 detections at $1024 \times 1024$ resolution (`conf = 0.30`) with dynamic rectangular stride-32 letterboxing (`auto=True, stride=32`), `Leaf_Segmenter` `retina_masks: true`, and `report.masks.subtract_holes: true`. I decode prototype masks natively via `ultra_rep.process_mask_native` and isolate the primary focal `Leaf` (`class_id == 0`) instance (`_largest_component >= 64` px) ranked by confidence and crop centrality alongside blade area, so overlapping neighbor leaf predictions inside the crop are never fused into the focal blade. I retain secondary pinna `Leaf` components (`>= 64` px) only when they are topologically bridged to the primary lamina through the `Petiole`/rachis mask (`class_id == 1`), union all `Petiole` masks to retain `_largest_component >= 64` px, and union all `Hole` masks while retaining all 8-connected components with `_components >= 64` px. In `_prepare_lamina_mask()` and `classify_and_route_leaf()`, I delegate mask preparation and contour extraction alongside closed-outline resampling and polygon morphology directly to native `LeafMachine3` core modules (`prepare_mask` and `extract_contour` alongside `resample_closed` and `polygon_morphology` alongside `length_width_from_box`), where `lamina = silhouette & ~hole` ensures that the 120-vertex outer contour traces the clean lamina silhouette. To veto merged multi-leaf or leaf-stem clumps, I evaluate LeafMachine3's Voronoi midvein bilateral symmetry (`orient_and_score_leaf` and `score_leaf`) and reject non-folded entire candidates with `bilateral_dice < 0.70` or `|si_a| >= 0.35` (and dissected candidates with `bilateral_dice < 0.45` or `|si_a| >= 0.48`).
* **31-Keypoint Anatomical Pose Estimation and Petiole/Landmark Delegation (`lm3_landmark_detector__yolo26x_pose_640`):** I pad each leaf crop with a 10% white border (`WHITE_PAD_FRAC = 0.10`, RGB `[255, 255, 255]`) and apply dynamic rectangular stride-32 letterboxing (`auto=True, stride=32`) at $640 \times 640$ resolution. I predict 31 anatomical keypoints (`mid15_pet5`) and normalize 1-based canonical keys (`midvein_1..15`, `petiole_1..5`, `petiole_6`) to distinct 0-based native `LeafMachine3` `KPT_NAMES` (`midvein_0..14`, `petiole_0..4`, `petiole_tip`) without key collisions. I delegate petiole skeleton geometry and landmark caliper widths directly to `leafmachine3.core.petiole.measure_petiole` and `leafmachine3.core.landmark_metrics.compute_measurements`.
* **Radial-and-Angular Euler Characteristic Transform (ECT) and Product Rendering:** I delegate $32 \times 360$ (`ect_num_dirs = 360, num_thresh = 32`) radial-and-angular Euler Characteristic Transform computation and visual product rendering directly to `leafmachine3.core.ect_compute.compute_ect` and `leafmachine3.reporting.ect_products.ect_product_for` alongside `leafmachine3.reporting.leaf_products.render_leaf_products`, which saves `.npy` matrices to `data/ect/*_ect.npy` and records `ect_path` in `extracted_leaves`.

### 3.2 Read-Only LeafMachine3 Ingestion, Organ Indexing and Scale Calibration (`scripts/pipeline/02_segment_and_extract.py`)
* **DBSCAN Plant Decomposition (`cluster_voucher_plants_dbscan`):** I cluster organs into individual plants (`plant_individual_id = 0, 1, ...`) and estimate rosette centroids alongside crown extents.
* **Rosette Caudex Crown Estimation (`estimate_rosette_crown_point`):** I identify the root-shoot transition zone ($p_{\text{crown}}$). I measure petiole length as outer-rosette geodesic distance:
  $$L_{\text{petiole}} = \max\left(d_{\text{geodesic}}(p_{\text{base}}, p_{\text{crown}}),\, \|p_{\text{base}} - p_{\text{crown}}\|_2\right)$$
  aggregated at $P_{75}$ across outer basal leaves. I evaluate scape height ($H_{\text{plant}}$) across both straight and bent stems:
  $$H_{\text{plant}} = \|p_{\text{bend}} - p_{\text{crown}}\|_2 + \|p_{\text{corymb}} - p_{\text{bend}}\|_2$$
* **Post-Segmentation Anatomical `leaf_zone` Classification (`classify_leaf_zone_anatomical`):** Because acropetal heteroblasty between petiolate basal rosette leaves and sessile or clasping cauline stem leaves is diagnostically informative across North American *Packera* (Barkley 1962; Trock 2006), I segment all valid leaves on each plant individual and classify each leaf's anatomical position (`leaf_zone = 'basal'` versus `'cauline'`, exported with `leaf_zone_confidence` and `dist_from_crown_norm`). This classifier combines normalized distance from the caudex crown (`p_crown`) along the plant axis (`p_crown -> p_corymb`) and relative vertical position (`cy_rel_plant_bbox`) alongside petiole attachment (`petiole_to_blade_ratio` and `Petiole` mask presence) and relative lamina area within the `plant_individual_id`.
* **Read-Only LeafMachine3 Persistence and Downstream Step 03 QC (`evaluate_step03_lm3_efa_qc`):** Step 02 operates as a read-only `LeafMachine3` ingestion adapter that persists 100% of segmented leaf instances, native Voronoi bilateral symmetry metrics (`bilateral_dice`, `si_a`), border-clipping indicators, 120-vertex tip-up `lamina_base`-anchored contours (`contour_xy_json`), and $32 \times 360$ ECT arrays (`data/ect/*.npy`) directly to SQLite `extracted_leaves`. Downstream clump and fragment exclusion executes deterministically inside Step 03 (`evaluate_step03_lm3_efa_qc`), so statistical QC thresholds can be audited or recalibrated across Tier B runs without repeating GPU segmentation.
* **Dissected-Leaf Rachis Bridging (`_build_native_lm3_crop_masks`):** In deeply pinnatifid leaves with narrow segmentation breaks, I retain secondary `Leaf` components (`>= 64` px) inside a crop only when they are topologically bridged to the primary focal lamina through the `Petiole`/rachis mask (`class_id == 1`) alongside `specimen_paper_cleaned_mask`. This topological constraint preserves pinnatisect lobes while preventing adjacent overlapping leaves at crop borders from fusing into a single contour.
* **300-DPI PNG Organ Montages and Native Visual Caches (`scripts/vision/step02_figures.py`):** Step 02 exports downstream-QC-filtered 300-DPI PNG plates (`fig02a_lm3_organ_and_ect_exemplars.png` and `fig02b_segmentation_qc_and_phenophase.png`), unlimited taxon-indexed `LeafMachine3` cutouts and polar ECT badges (`lm3_assets/` and `ect_overlays/`), and capped whole-sheet diagnostic overlays (`lm3_sheet_overlays/`, `max_sheets = 24`).
* **Segment and Run Reset (`purge_segments_and_runs`):** When I invoke the `purge-segments` (`purge_segments` or `purge_old_data`) CLI control in `run_pipeline.sh`, `main.py`, or `02_segment_and_extract.py`, `purge_segments_and_runs()` in `scripts/core/db.py` clears all Step 02 to 06 masks, 120-vertex contours, ECT matrices, organ crops, downstream CSV tables, run directories (`runs/`), and SQLite Step 02 to 06 rows (followed by `VACUUM`), while it preserves Step 01 `vouchers`, `taxon_synonyms`, `environmental_cache`, `data/raw_vouchers/`, and `data/tables/curated_vouchers.csv`.

By partitioning basal rosette and cauline stem leaves into explicit anatomical zones while I reject severed fragments and mounting tape artifacts, I preserve developmental homology across both foliar series.

## 4. Phase 3 and 4 Protocol: Morphometrics, EFA, Topological ECT, Allometry and Ordination (`03` and `04`)

In Phase 3 and Phase 4, I transform discrete leaf outlines into continuous mathematical and topological functions. At first glance, elliptic Fourier analysis and Euler Characteristic Transform topology seem different, but that difference is superficial because both approaches quantify boundary and sinus variance relative to biological homology.

### 4.1 Shape Quantification, Strict `Tier_1_Direct` EFA, $32 \times 360$ ECT Decomposition and Dual Basal/Cauline Tracks (`03_fourier_extractor.R` and `dissected_leaf_utils.R`)
* **Strict `Tier_1_Direct` Closed-Contour Restriction and Anatomical Zone Partitioning (`classify_and_partition_leaf_zones`):**
  I restrict `Momocs::efourier` strictly to intact `Tier_1_Direct` contours and never pool folded or reflected `Tier_2_Reflected` leaves into EFA. I ingest contours through batch SQL from `extracted_leaves.contour_xy_json` across both `leaf_zone == 'basal'` and `leaf_zone == 'cauline'` blades, and I persist all individual leaf harmonic vectors and their `leaf_zone` tags in `leaf_efa_harmonics` alongside `leaf_efa_harmonics_individual.csv`.
* **Landmark-Anchored Tip-Up Homology and Harmonic Power Calibration:**
  I center and isotropically scale `lamina_base -> lamina_tip` homologized contours via `Momocs::coo_center() %>% Momocs::coo_scale()` (with horizontal major-axis alignment `align_contour_horizontal_xax` prior to `rm_asym`), which preserves true length-to-width aspect ratios below $1.0$ on reniform and cordate blades. Harmonic power is defined as:
  $$\text{Power}_n = \frac{A_n^2 + B_n^2 + C_n^2 + D_n^2}{2},\quad \frac{\sum_{n=1}^{H_{\text{opt}}} \text{Power}_n}{\sum_{n=1}^{H_{\max}} \text{Power}_n} \ge 0.99$$
  I dynamically determine the harmonic count $H_{\text{opt}} \in [6, 24]$ that captures $\ge 99\%$ cumulative power, and I execute `Momocs::efourier(out_obj, nb.h = H_opt, norm = FALSE, start = FALSE)`.
* **Uniform Bilateral Symmetrization (Claude 2008):**
  $$A_n^{\text{sym}} = A_n,\quad D_n^{\text{sym}} = D_n,\quad B_n^{\text{sym}} = 0,\quad C_n^{\text{sym}} = 0,\quad \text{pct\_asymmetry} = \frac{\sum_{n=1}^{H_{\text{opt}}} (B_n^2 + C_n^2)}{\sum_{n=1}^{H_{\text{opt}}} (A_n^2 + B_n^2 + C_n^2 + D_n^2)}$$
* **Topological Euler Characteristic Transform (ECT) Alignment, Symmetrization and SVD (`compute_leaf_ect_features`):**
  Because Fourier harmonics can smooth deep pinnatisect sinuses and fail on multi-island compound blades, `compute_leaf_ect_features()` in `scripts/morphometrics/dissected_leaf_utils.R` loads each leaf's $32 \times 360$ radial-and-angular ECT matrix from `data/ect/*_ect.npy` (or computes the exact EmbeddedGraph cycle Euler characteristic $\chi(\phi_j, t_i) = \#V(h \le t_i) - \#E(\max(h_u, h_v) \le t_i)$ across 360 angular directions and 32 radial thresholds via `compute_cycle_ect_r()`; Ayub et al. 2026; Weaver 2026). Column 1 is anchored to the leaf apex ($\phi = 0$), and directional columns $2..\text{nc}$ are reflected to form the bilateral symmetric half-matrix $\mathbf{E}_{\text{sym}} = \frac{1}{2}(\mathbf{E} + \mathbf{E}_{\text{refl}})$ under a signed log-modulus transform $\operatorname{sign}(\mathbf{E}_{\text{sym}})\log(1 + |\mathbf{E}_{\text{sym}}|)$. From each matrix, I compute bilateral ECT asymmetry (`ect_bilateral_asym`) and topological lobe complexity (`ect_lobe_complexity` and `cauline_ect_lobe_complexity`) as the sum of excess directional total variation $\operatorname{mean}_j(\max(0, (\text{TV}_j - 2)/2))$ and multi-component sinus excess $\operatorname{mean}(\max(0, \chi - 1))$. I then extract authority-weighted SVD principal components anchored on active basal leaves (`ECT_PC1..PC3`, positively aligned with `ect_lobe_complexity`) alongside orthogonal cauline ECT components (`Cauline_ECT_PC1..PC2`) and within-plant basal-to-cauline topological divergence (`heteroblasty_ect_distance`).
* **Type Outlier Immunity, Pure Basal Primary OTU Aggregation and Orthogonal Cauline Track:**
  I exempt intact type contours from within-taxon Mahalanobis and MCD outlier screening (`is_contour_outlier[is_type_specimen] <- FALSE`). Active type anchors receive maximum authority precision in `stats::cov.wt()`. I export PCs accounting for $\ge 95\%$ variance ($k_{\text{export}} = \max(5, k_{95})$). During coordinate-wise OTU aggregation to `(catalogNumber, plant_individual_id)` (`aggregate_step03_otu_medians_fast()` and `reconcile_step03_basal_otu_medians()`), I compute unweighted primary OTU medians (`A1..D_H`, `PC1..PCk`, `ECT_PC1..PC3`, `ect_lobe_complexity`, `solidity`, `dissection_index`) strictly across clean `leaf_zone == 'basal'` leaves whenever $\ge 1$ basal leaf is present on the specimen (falling back to cauline medians as `Cauline_Only_Fallback` only when a specimen has 0 basal leaves), so cauline bracts never contaminate the basal rosette outline. Simultaneously, I aggregate `leaf_zone == 'cauline'` leaves into an orthogonal cauline and heteroblasty track (`cauline_leaf_count`, `cauline_solidity_med`, `cauline_dissection_index_med`, `cauline_aspect_ratio_med`, `cauline_blade_length_mm`, `cauline_ect_lobe_complexity`, `basal_vs_cauline_length_ratio`, `basal_vs_cauline_solidity_delta`, `heteroblasty_efa_distance`, `heteroblasty_ect_distance`, `Cauline_PC1..PC3`, and `Cauline_ECT_PC1..PC2`).

### 4.2 Determiner Authority Weighting, Spatial Population Demes and Reference Anchors (`authority_weighting_utils.R`, `plasticity_mitigation_utils.R`)
* **Hierarchical Heteroblasty Partitioning, 10 km Spatial Demes and Plasticity Mitigation:**
  To account for within-plant heteroblasty and local microsite plasticity without pre-shrinking feature coordinates before cross-validation or MANOVA, I separate variance sources across three explicit stages:
  - **Level 1 (Orthogonal Basal and Cauline Heteroblasty Partitioning):** Primary OTU coordinates represent unweighted basal rosette medians (`reconcile_step03_basal_otu_medians()`), whereas acropetal leaf dimorphism is modeled simultaneously through the orthogonal cauline and heteroblasty feature track (`Cauline_PC1..PC3`, `Cauline_ECT_PC1..PC2`, `basal_vs_cauline_solidity_delta`, `heteroblasty_ect_distance`).
  - **Level 2 (10 km Conspecific Spatial Deme Assignment):** In `assign_spatial_gathering_demes()`, conspecific individuals within a $10\text{ km}$ single-linkage Haversine radius (and shared gathering keys) receive deterministic `population_deme_id` and `deme_n_specimens` identifiers while preserving raw specimen morphometric coordinates for independent LOOCV and MANOVA hypothesis testing.
  - **Level 3 (Within-Deme Phenophase Residualization and Intraspecific $Q_{ST}$ Whitening):** `residualize_within_deme_phenophase_plasticity()` removes within-deme phenological drift (`pheno_sin`, `pheno_cos`), and `whiten_intraspecific_deme_plasticity()` whitens intraspecific clustering against pooled within-deme environmental scatter before applying ridgeline/spatial-segregation merging (`merge_plastic_gmm_components_ridgeline()`).
* **Two-Stage Gold/Silver Anchor Seeding, Dynamic Heteroblastic Sub-Modes and Confident Learning Pruning:**
  In `select_adaptive_reference_anchors()`, taxa with $\ge \max(5, p_{\text{eff}} + 3)$ `Tier_1_Gold` + `Tier_0_Type` anchors seed the initial FastMCD reference ellipsoid on Gold and Type specimens ($\chi^2_{0.95, p}$; minimum sample size $n \ge \max(12, 5p)$ per Rousseeuw & Van Driessen 1999) and screen candidate `Tier_2_Silver` vouchers against that envelope at $\chi^2_{0.90, p}$ before refitting the combined anchor covariance. When a taxon exhibits bimodal basal leaf dissection across its `Tier_1_Gold` anchors ($\ge 2$ dissected and $\ge 2$ entire Gold vouchers with basal solidity span $> 0.15$), sub-mode FastMCD screening evaluates whole and dissected morphotypes jointly without static species lists. Active anchors are screened via leave-one-out shrinkage discriminant posteriors $\hat{P}_{-i}(y = k \mid x_i)$ with per-class self-confidence thresholds $t_j$ (Northcutt et al. 2021). Anchors that violate their assigned class threshold while exceeding another class's threshold are flagged (`is_confident_label_noise = TRUE`), demoted to `"Passive_Confident_Noise_Demoted"`, and projected passively. Intact types receive retention immunity (`anchor_tier_source = "Tier_0_Type_Anchor"`), whereas extreme types ($> \chi^2_{0.99}$) are isolated from FastMCD scatter estimation. Taxon reference centers shrink toward the type medoid:
  $$\boldsymbol{\mu}_{\text{ref}, k} \leftarrow (1 - \gamma_k) \boldsymbol{\mu}_{\text{ref}, k} + \gamma_k \boldsymbol{\mu}_{\text{type}, k},\quad \gamma_k = \min\!\left(0.35,\, \frac{2.5\,n_{\text{eff, type}, k}}{N_{\text{gs}, k} + 2.5\,n_{\text{eff, type}, k}}\right)$$
  Taxa with $< 8$ Gold and Silver anchors promote central `Tier_3_Bronze` vouchers within $\chi^2_{0.80}$ (`is_promoted_anchor = TRUE`).
* **Crossed-Effects Hierarchical Bayesian Authority Weights:**
  `compute_empirical_bayes_authority_weights()` evaluates leave-one-out PC concordance (`use_loo_pc`) unconditionally when $G \ge 2$, and it combines determiner reliability $\theta^{\text{det}}_d$ with herbarium reliability $\theta^{\text{inst}}_h = \frac{4.0 \cdot 0.85 + \sum_{i \in h} c_i}{4.0 + n_h}$ in log-odds space ($\alpha_0 = 5.0$, prior reliabilities $\pi_{\text{PrimaryType}} = 0.999$, $\pi_{\text{SecType}} = 0.985$, $\pi_{\text{Gold}} = 0.95$, $\pi_{\text{Silver}} = 0.85$, $\pi_{\text{PromotedBronze}} = 0.65$, $\pi_{\text{Bronze}} = 0.35$; Dawid & Skene 1979; Gelman & Hill 2007). I update each specimen's authority weight via Raykar et al. (2010) multiplicative Bayesian posterior updating against its leave-one-out cross-modal likelihood $L_{\text{LOO}}(\mathbf{x}_i \mid y_i^{\text{obs}})$:
  $$w_i = \frac{\pi_{d(i), h(i)} \, L_{\text{LOO}}(\mathbf{x}_i \mid y_i^{\text{obs}})}{\pi_{d(i), h(i)} \, L_{\text{LOO}}(\mathbf{x}_i \mid y_i^{\text{obs}}) + (1 - \pi_{d(i), h(i)}) \, \bar{L}_{\text{LOO}}(\mathbf{x}_i \mid k \ne y_i^{\text{obs}})}$$
  Types remain exempt from downward shrinkage, and unpromoted Bronze vouchers receive $0.0$ in `active_passive` mode.

### 4.3 Klingenberg Allometry, Dual-Track CDA and MANOVA (`04_gmm_morphotools.R`)
* **Two-Sample Prediction-Variance Corrected Type Distance and Per-Species Canonical GMM Relabeling:**
  $$D^2_{\text{type}, i} = \left(1 + \frac{1}{n_{\text{eff, type}, k}}\right)^{-1} (\mathbf{x}_i - \boldsymbol{\mu}_{\text{type}, k})^\top \mathbf{S}_{\text{reg}}^{-1} (\mathbf{x}_i - \boldsymbol{\mu}_{\text{type}, k})$$
  persisted as `type_morph_mahal_d2`, alongside `nearest_type_morph_taxon`, basionym-level `nearest_type_basionym` and `nearest_type_basionym_d2` (preserving distinct centroids per `species_raw` basionym so homotypic *sensu stricto* types anchor the taxon baseline while heterotypic types identify available resurrection names), and per-species `is_typical_morphotype` (GMM cluster $c^*_k$). Because Gaussian Mixture Model component indices are arbitrary across runs, `.canonical_relabel_gmm_clusters()` relabels `mclust` mixture components so Cluster 1 ($c^* \equiv 1$) is always the component nearest the homotypic nomenclatural type medoid or highest-weighted `Tier_1_Gold` core and orders secondary components $2..G$ by Mahalanobis distance from Cluster 1, whereas `compute_type_specimen_morph_control()` evaluates `is_typical_morphotype` per species ($c^*_k$) across multi-species runs (Scrucca et al. 2016; Cadena et al. 2018). `BayesFactor_vs_Null` scales $2\Delta\text{BIC}$ by Kish's effective sample size ratio $N_{\text{eff}} / N_{\text{core}}$.
* **Klingenberg (2016) Allometric Slope Homogeneity:**
  I evaluate slope homogeneity across ruler-calibrated anchors (`is_calibrated_mm == TRUE`) through multivariate interaction models in `RRPP::lm.rrpp(..., weights = eff_weight)`:
  $$\mathbf{y}_{\text{shape}} = \beta_0 + \beta_1 \log_{10}(\text{CS}) + \boldsymbol{\gamma}_{\text{species}} + \boldsymbol{\delta} (\log_{10}(\text{CS}) \times \boldsymbol{\gamma}_{\text{species}}) + \boldsymbol{\epsilon}$$
  When interaction slopes are homogeneous ($p_{\text{interaction}} > 0.05$) and allometric size accounts for meaningful shape variance ($R^2_{\text{size}} \ge 0.10$ in `"auto"` mode), I apply common-slope residual correction: $\mathbf{y}_{\text{corrected}} = \mathbf{y}_{\text{shape}} - \hat{\beta}_1 (\log_{10}(\text{CS}) - \overline{\log_{10}(\text{CS})})$.
* **Subspace Selection, Modality-Independent Deme-Blocked CDA, and *A Priori* MANOVA Gating:**
  CDA and GMM feature pools incorporate `aspect_ratio`, `petiole_to_blade_ratio`, `AR_inv`, and scale-free inflorescence branching `log1p_capitulum_count` (on reproductive plants with `capitula_count >= 1`) alongside LeafMachine3 eroded-lamina paper-normalized CIE $L^*$ vestiture metrics (`blade_cie_l_norm`, `blade_cie_l_sd`), `basal_vs_cauline_length_ratio`, `basal_vs_cauline_solidity_delta`, `heteroblasty_efa_distance`, `heteroblasty_ect_distance`, `cauline_solidity_med`, `cauline_dissection_index_med`, `cauline_ect_lobe_complexity`, `Cauline_PC1..PC3`, `ECT_PC1..PC3`, `Cauline_ECT_PC1..PC2`, `ect_lobe_complexity`, and `dissection_index` alongside `solidity` (pruned for collinearity at $|r| \le 0.75$). I apply Horn's Parallel Analysis capped by Williams & Titus (1988) $P_{\max} = \lfloor (N_{\min,\text{anchor}} - 1) / 3 \rfloor$. In `compute_weighted_shrinkage_cda_loocv()`, I evaluate strictly morphological, taxon-blind 10 km gathering/deme-blocked cross-validation with inside-fold feature selection and weighted Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage CDA (Ledoit & Wolf 2004), keeping spatial coordinates completely excluded from morphological posteriors. Track 1 evaluates Unsupervised GMM Morphotypes (`Morphotype_K1..Kn`) via Kass & Raftery (1995) $2\Delta\text{BIC}$ thresholds ($> 6$ strong, $> 10$ very strong) while suppressing post-selection supervised CV accuracy and parametric `gmm_cluster` MANOVA $p$-values (Cadena et al. 2018; Gao et al. 2024). Track 2 evaluates Supervised EPA Level III Ecoregion CDA biplots and *a priori* MANOVA (`species_standardized` and `regional_group`) with Benjamini-Hochberg FDR.
* **Open-Set Recognition (OSR) Class-Conditional Mahalanobis Novelty Scoring:**
  Closed-set discriminant posteriors force every specimen into a training taxon even when a sheet belongs to an unmodeled congener or novel lineage. To relax the closed-world assumption, `.compute_open_set_recognition_metrics()` computes class-conditional Mahalanobis distances in regularized morphospace across all candidate congeneric reference centroids in `data/packera_pipeline.sqlite` (Scheirer et al. 2013; Lee et al. 2018; Geng et al. 2020), and it exports the minimum congener distance `min_congener_mahal_d2` and closest congener `min_congener_taxon` alongside upper-tail chi-square novelty probability `open_set_novelty_p` and confident label-noise flag `is_confident_label_noise` to `otu_morphometrics` and `morphometric_flags.csv`.

## 5. Phase 5 Protocol: Multimodal Spatial RF, *Locus Classicus* Ecological Control and SDM Ensembles (`05_multimodal_spatial_rf.R`)

Historical collections frequently lack fine-scale GPS coordinates, as eighteenth and nineteenth-century collectors recorded only general county or river valley descriptions. If an ecological model extracts raster values directly at an uncertain county centroid, the extracted soil variables become distorted.

* **Type Locality (*Locus Classicus*) Ecological Baseline:**
  I gate direct point-raster extraction on types by coordinate uncertainty ($\le 2000\text{ m}$ edaphic and topographic, $\le 10000\text{ m}$ climatic; Daru et al. 2018). Coarse type sheets derive their *locus classicus* edaphic profiles from proximate Gold vouchers within 150 km or sharing the EPA Level III ecoregion. I calculate physiological divergence through weighted RMS distance:
  $$z_{\text{type}, i} = \sqrt{\frac{\sum_{j \in \text{Primary}} w_j \left(\frac{x_{i, j} - \mu_{\text{type}, k, j}}{\max(\text{MAD}_{\text{type}, k, j},\, s_{\text{floor}, j},\, 10^{-4})}\right)^2}{\sum_{j \in \text{Primary}} w_j}}$$
  exported as `type_edaphic_z_score`, alongside `nearest_type_niche_taxon` and boolean `matches_type_lithology`.
* **Full 19-Bioclimatic and 0 to 15 cm Rhizosphere Stack:**
  I extract 19 WorldClim v2.1 bioclimatic variables (`bio1` to `bio19`) and 0 to 15 cm rhizosphere depth-weighting (`(5 * v_0_5 + 10 * v_5_15) / 15`) across SSURGO and 30 m POLARIS alongside 100 m SOLUS100 and 3DEP topography with 6-class USGS SGMC lithology (`edaphic_fine_grain_utils.R`).
* **3-Stage Dynamic Primary Predictor Gating:**
  First, I prune collinear predictors ($|r| < 0.70$, $\text{VIF} < 5.0$) across Climatic and Edaphic blocks alongside Topographic variables. Second, I train `ranger::ranger` under $0.5^\circ \times 0.5^\circ$ Spatial Block CV with continuous Empirical Bayes weights (primary types pinned to 3.0 ceiling) and winsorized case weights `[0.25, 4.0]`, which isolates variables accounting for the top 85% cumulative importance. Third, I restrict downstream niche distances strictly to discoverable primary predictors.
* **SDM Ensembles (MaxEnt and Spatial RF):**
  I fit authority-weighted MaxEnt (`maxnet`, `cloglog` link; `step05_sdm_maxent.R`) and Spatial RF ensembles with target-group background selection, and I export spatial-block $AUC_{\text{block}}$ and Continuous Boyce Index (CBI) to reports and GeoTIFFs (`fig05a_ecological_niche_and_rf_importance.png`, `fig05b_hopkins_circular_phenology.png`, `fig05c_sdm_suitability_and_distribution_map.png`).
* **Hopkins Circular Phenology (`step05_phenology_ecotypes.R`):**
  I regress circular anthesis DOY ($\theta_i = \frac{2\pi \cdot \text{DOY}_i}{365.25}$) on latitude and elevation alongside temperature, scaled by $\alpha = \min(1.0, \max(0.0, (N_{\text{anthesis}} - 5)/15))$. Flowering dispersion scales thresholds ($\tau_{\text{cryptic}} = \min(35, \max(14, 2.5\,\sigma_{\text{DOY}}))$, $\tau_{\text{ecotype}} = \min(28, \max(10, 2.0\,\sigma_{\text{DOY}}))$).
* **Step 05 Passive Projection of Missing GMM Clusters:**
  In `resolve_analysis_group()`, the model preserves `is_missing_gmm_cluster` so non-Tier-1 specimens lacking a Step 04 `gmm_cluster` are excluded from Step 05 Spatial RF training anchors and predicted passively.

## 6. Phase 6 Protocol: Reliability-Weighted Consensus and Hierarchical Nomenclatural Type Decision Matrix (`06_triage_dashboard_synthesis.R`)

In Phase 6, I synthesize all lines of evidence. If for say, a specimen displays an anomalous leaf shape but matches its type locality in soils and phenology, a single-modality model would misclassify it as a new species. By synthesizing across independent data streams, I resolve genuine taxonomic status.

* **Master View Synthesis and Reliability-Weighted Consensus:**
  I synthesize consensus by querying `v_integrative_triage_master`, which joins all modalities onto `plant_individuals`. I weight modalities by out-of-fold Cohen's $\kappa_m$ and class-conditional precision $C^{(m)}_{k,j}$:
  $$w_{i,m} = w_m \cdot (0.5 + 0.5 \cdot \text{Precision}_m(\hat{y}_{i,m}))$$
  which achieves high voucher completion across georeferenced and non-georeferenced sheets.
* **Taxon-Agnostic 5-Stage Hierarchical Decision Matrix:**
  - **Sibling Ramet Morphology Propagation and Same-Sheet Gathering Coherence (`catalogNumber`):** On multi-plant herbarium sheets, leafless or flowering-only ramets (`plant_individual_id`) inherit measured foliar morphology from conspecific sibling ramets on the same `catalogNumber` sheet before decision scoring, and I enforce same-sheet (`catalogNumber`) gathering coherence across sibling `plant_individual_id` ramets so ramets from a single gathering receive consistent taxonomic status calls.
  - **Rule A (Nomenclatural Type Override):** I never reassign type specimens (`recommended_determination = given_sp`), nor do I route them to `Misidentification_Reassign` or `Hybrid_Introgressant` alongside `Putative_Cryptic_Species`. Concordant types receive `taxonomic_status_call = "Confirmed_Type_Control"` (`RESOLVED`). Cross-modal divergence triggers `taxonomic_status_call = "Type_Synonymy_Candidate"` (`CRITICAL`).
  - **Rule B and Stage 1 Precedence (`Misidentification_Reassign` and Relative Open-Set Congener Reassignment):** `apply_taxonomic_decision_matrix()` evaluates Stage 1 misidentification and Open-Set congener reassignment (`congener_osr_reassign`) strictly before Stage 3 cryptic lineage routing, where it applies calibrated CDA thresholds (`0.72` / `0.68` / `0.62`) and a radial intraspecific edaphic Schoener's $D$ fallback $\exp(-0.5 \cdot (z_{\text{edaph}} / 1.8)^2)$. For `congener_osr_reassign`, I require relative Mahalanobis gating in $p$-dimensional morphospace: $D^2_{\text{Mahal}}(k') \le \chi^2_{0.90, p}$, $D^2_{\text{Mahal}}(k') < 0.70 \cdot D^2_{\text{Mahal}}(y_i)$, and $D^2_{\text{Mahal}}(y_i) > \chi^2_{0.95, p}$ for a congener $k' \ne y_i$. Under Rule B, I enforce orthogonal 1-vote-per-modality consensus (`vote_morph` + `vote_edaph`) so correlated morphological or ecological streams cannot double-count a single modality, and I protect specimens with concordant leaf morphology (`cda_predicted_taxon == given_sp` with `cda_prob >= 0.65`) from edaphic-only `Misidentification_Reassign` overrides.
  - **Rule C (*Sensu Stricto* Typical Morphotype Locking):** Specimens in their species' canonically relabeled typical morphotype GMM cluster ($c^*_k$) with $z_{\text{type}} \le 2.0$ are locked as `Confirmed_Species` (*sensu stricto*).
  - **Stage 2 (`Hybrid_Introgressant` via 5-Gate Population Contact-Zone Swarm Protocol):** I disable interspecific hybrid evaluation completely in `--intraspecific-mode` and whenever fewer than 2 standardized *Packera* species are present in the cohort. In multi-species runs, I require five joint gates before elevating any non-type basal-rosette voucher to `Hybrid_Introgressant`: (1) verified physical syntopy of both parent species within `15 km` (or `25 km` within the same county), (2) overlapping Hopkins-adjusted flowering windows (`21 days` or less peak separation) and shared microhabitat tolerance (`edaphic_z <= 2.5`), (3) pairwise parental Mahalanobis chord projection $t_{AB} \in [0.22, 0.78]$ with orthogonal residual $d^2_{\perp} \le \chi^2_{0.90, p-1}$, (4) multi-organ corroboration across leaf outline and vestiture reflectance, involucre geometry, or foliar fluctuating asymmetry alongside non-certain posterior split, and (5) population-level contact-zone replication (`hybrid_zone_cohesion_pass == TRUE`, requiring at least 2 independent admixed vouchers in the local contact zone for the same parent pair unless explicitly annotated with a hybrid cross on the herbarium label). Confirmed hybrid swarm vouchers export explicit putative parent binomials in `hybrid_parent_1` and `hybrid_parent_2` alongside `recommended_determination` (`Parent 1 x Parent 2`).
  - **Stage 3 (`Putative_Cryptic_Species` and Population-Cohesion Lineage Gate):** To prevent isolated taphonomic outliers or local population structure from being over-split into spurious species, I enforce a **Population-Cohesion Lineage Gate** (`cryptic_cluster_cohesion_pass`; Carstens et al. 2013; Sukumaran & Knowles 2017; Fišer et al. 2018). A specimen in a secondary morphotype cluster ($g_{\text{cl}} > 1$) can only be routed to `Putative_Cryptic_Species` when its cluster satisfies `cryptic_cluster_cohesion_pass == TRUE`:
    1. $\ge 5$ independent collection events (`recordedBy + year + county/lat`) across $\ge 2$ collectors or herbaria;
    2. Cluster-median `min_congener_mahal_d2` $> \chi^2_{0.95, p}$ (`open_set_novelty_p < 0.05` against all known congeners);
    3. $\ge 50\%$ of cluster members share concordant ecological ($D_{\text{edaph}} < 0.30$ or $z_{\text{edaph}} > 2.5$) or phenological ($|\Delta\text{DOY}_{\text{resid}}| \ge \tau_{\text{cryptic}}$) divergence with $\text{BF} \ge 10.0$.
    Non-cohesive secondary-cluster specimens route to `Confirmed_Species` (*sensu lato*) or `Ecophenotypic_Plasticity` alongside `Subspecies_Ecotype`.
  - **Stage 4 (`Subspecies_Ecotype` vs. `Ecophenotypic_Plasticity`):** Evaluates cohesive geographic or edaphic divergence for ecotypes versus overlapping envelopes for plasticity.
* **Synthesis Figures and Curatorial Reports:**
  I generate integrative multi-panel plates (`scripts/figures/step06_figures.R`; `fig06a_integrative_taxonomic_synthesis.png` and `Figure_Integrative_<Focal_Taxon>_Revision.png`) and produce actionable curation summaries (`Packera_Taxonomic_Revision_Summary.md`, `triage_queue.csv`, `Triage_Dashboard_Interactive.html`, `curation_triage`).

### Quantitative Taxon-Complex Delimitation Within Phase 6 (`taxon_complex_discovery_utils.R`)

Within Phase 6, I also scale beyond single-species comparisons to delimit species complexes across the genus. At first glance, local ecotypes and distinct species complexes seem to require different analytical approaches, but that difference is superficial because both represent continuous divergence surfaces across morphospace and ecological landscapes.

* **Multimodal Pairwise Affinity Calculation:**
  I calculate pairwise morphological and fine-scale edaphic distances alongside climatic and phenological distances across all active taxa, and I shrink taxon centroids toward nomenclatural type baselines with weight $\gamma_k$.
* **Unsupervised Graph Modularity and Hierarchical Delimitation:**
  I discover species complexes through unsupervised graph modularity (Leiden and Louvain algorithms) and Ward.D2 hierarchical clustering, which resolves Taxon by GMM Morphotype subclusters when $N_{\text{taxa}} \le 2$.
* **Complex Discovery Plates and Neighbor Exports (`taxon_complex_discovery_utils.R`):**
  I export affinity networks and hierarchical dendrograms (`fig06b_taxon_complex_delimitation_network.png`) alongside pairwise distance summaries and neighbor lists (`empirical_taxon_complexes.csv`, `pairwise_taxon_affinity_matrix.csv`, `focal_auto_neighbors.txt`), which provides quantitative evidence for species complex boundaries.
