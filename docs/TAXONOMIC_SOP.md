## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure establishes the protocol for an **Automated High-Throughput Vegetative Phenotyping and Herbarium Triage Pipeline** applied to the taxonomically recalcitrant ***Packera dubia* (Spreng.) Trock & Mabb. complex** (Asteraceae: Senecioneae) across Eastern and Central North America.

The pipeline operationalizes morphological diagnosability within the theoretical framework of the **Unified Species Concept (USC; de Queiroz 2007)**. Under the USC, species are conceptualized as separately evolving metapopulation lineages; morphological diagnosability, reproductive isolation, ecological divergence, and monophyly serve as secondary operational criteria that arise contingently along the speciation continuum. Because vegetative morphology in *Packera* has historically been obscured by phenotypic plasticity, developmental heterophylly, and pervasive herbarium misidentifications, automated high-throughput vegetative morphometrics serves as a rigorous, objective screening and triage engine.

Vegetative phenotyping is not interpreted in isolation, but is explicitly evaluated in concert with:
1. **Reproductive Macro-Morphology:** Capitulum aspect ratios (involucre height to width), phyllary series and count, and ray/disc floret dimensions.
2. **Cytotaxonomic Cytotypes:** Chromosome numbers and polyploid series ($2n = 44, 46, 88$; Kowal 1975) that characterize distinct evolutionary lineages in southeastern Senecioneae.
3. **Reduced-Representation nextRAD Phylogenomics:** High-density single nucleotide polymorphism (SNP) datasets capable of discerning deep divergence, hybrid reticulation, and ancestral polymorphism.
4. **Micro-Edaphic Validation:** Regional 250m SoilGrids pedological rasters complemented by fine-scale 1:24,000 USDA NRCS SSURGO vector map units resolving localized edaphic endemics (e.g., granitic flatrocks, calcareous glades, ultramafic barrens).

## 2. Two-Stage Organ Detection, Four-Tiered Basal Leaf Extraction & Symmetric EFA Protocol

To resolve severe foliar crowding and press damage in herbarium rosettes without discarding irreplaceable vouchers, candidate organs are extracted via a **two-stage LeafMachine2 + PointRend + SAM 2 architecture** (Weaver et al. 2024) and routed through a **100% label-blind 4-tiered geometric hierarchy**:

- **Stage 1 (Full-Sheet Detection & Ruler Calibration):** Stock LeafMachine2 YOLOv5 `LeafPriority.pt` (11-class organ detector) processes both `Class 0` (`leaf_whole`) and `Class 1` (`leaf_partial`) candidate boxes to capture rosette-attached basal blades on native-DPI (`3753x5634`) scans, while pre-filtering upper-sheet cauline boxes (`cy < 0.40 * sheet_h`) and tiny fragments (`max(bh, bw) < int(200 * scale_f)`). Paired with the 38-class Ruler Detector (`scripts/vision/lm2_data_loader.py`) for empirical scale conversions (`pixels_per_mm`, `is_calibrated_mm`).
- **Stage 2 (Cropped Leaf Segmentation & SAM 2 Refinement):** Candidate leaf bounding boxes are cropped at native DPI with 12% padding, segmented via fine-tuned Detectron2 PointRend (`leaf_seg_rcnn_v2_1`), refined at sub-pixel margin resolution via automated prompt-guided SAM 2 (`--sam2-refine`), and spatially clustered into distinct individuals via DBSCAN (`plant_individual_id`).
- **Post-Gatekeeper Basal-Leaf Filtering & Top-6 Voucher Cap:** High-DPI basal candidates enforce `cy_rel >= 0.42`, minimum area floors (`>= 12000 * scale_f^2` for Tier 1, `>= 18000 * scale_f^2` and `cy_rel >= 0.48` for Tier 2), boxy clump rejection (`min(w,h)/max(w,h) > 0.78` when `solidity < 0.84`), sheet-relative area floor (`>= 0.28 * max_sheet_area`), and a top-6 basal leaves per voucher cap ranked by $Q = \text{solidity} \cdot \text{ucs} \cdot \sqrt{\text{area}}$ with automatic disk cleanup of dropped mask and contour files.

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A0["Stage 1: Full-Sheet YOLOv5 LeafPriority.pt (Class 0 & 1)\n+ 38-Class Ruler Calibration (lm2_data_loader.py)"] --> A["Stage 2: 12%-Padded Native-DPI Leaf Crop\n-> PointRend (leaf_seg_rcnn_v2_1) + SAM 2 Refinement"]
    A --> B{"Label-Blind Gatekeeper:\nSolidity >= 0.72 (or >= 0.50 via\nis_botanical_dissection) & UCS >= 0.85?"}
    B -- Yes --> C["Tier 1 (Tier_1_Direct): Pristine / Dissected Silhouette\n(Base-to-Apex Homologized 12-Harmonic EFA)"]
    B -- No --> D{"Intact Half-Blade or\nClean Midrib Fold?"}
    D -- Yes --> E["Tier 2 (Tier_2_Reflected): Midrib Reflection\n(Synthesize Bilateral Silhouette for EFA)"]
    D -- No --> F{"Measurable Margin\nCurvature?"}
    F -- Yes --> G["Tier 3: Open Curve Analysis\n(Chebyshev Polynomials & Landmark Traits)"]
    F -- No --> H["Tier 4: Holistic Rosette & Organ Crops\n(rosettes_dense/ -> DINOv2 768-d Embeddings)"]
```

1. **Tier 1 (`"Tier_1_Direct"` — Direct Pristine & Label-Blind Dissected Extraction):** Fully intact basal leaves meeting Solidity $\ge 0.72$ and Unoccluded Completeness Score ($\text{UCS} \ge 0.85$) are segmented directly into closed binary masks after automated petiole trimming (`trim_slender_petiole()`, `trim_petiole_tail`, with broad cordate base protection). Resolution-adaptive cross-sectional width profiling (`w_span = max(min(max(h, w), 350), int(0.65 * max(h, w)))` in `_sample_cross_sectional_widths()` and `trim_petiole_tail()`) ensures native-DPI basal blades ($>700\text{ px}$ wide) are never truncated, while resolution-scaled petiole width thresholds (`eff_max_petiole_w` when `axis_len > 450.0` in `_disambiguate_base_apex_endpoints()`, and `max_petiole_width = max(16.0, 0.105 * blade_width_est)` / `cordate_guard_w = max(..., 0.135 * blade_width_est)` when `blade_width_est > 250.0 and min(h, w) > 500` in `trim_petiole_tail()`) preserve exact backward compatibility on $400 \times 400$ synthetic unit-test fixtures. Spatial rosette centroids (`rosette_centroids_by_pid` in `02_segment_and_extract.py`) are wired via `rosette_centroid` into `classify_and_route_leaf()` and `compute_geometric_metrics()`. Low-solidity leaves ($0.50 \le \text{Solidity} < 0.72$) are evaluated by `is_botanical_dissection()`, a **100% label-blind** geometric gatekeeper (with legacy `taxon_has_lyrate_tendency` completely removed) that verifies paired bilateral convexity-defect sinuses ($d_{\text{sinus}} / W_{\text{blade}} \ge 0.08$) and smooth non-jagged sinus boundaries to admit genuinely pinnatifid/lyrate blades while rejecting torn or occluded leaves.
2. **Tier 2 (`"Tier_2_Reflected"` — Hemi-Blade Bilateral Symmetry Reflection):** Leaves folded cleanly along the midrib (`detect_folded_leaf()` requiring `has_diagonal_crease and solidity < 0.72`) or partially occluded leaves preserving an intact half-blade from `p_base` to `p_apex` are cleaved along the midrib vector and reflected across that axis in OpenCV to reconstruct a synthetic bilateral silhouette.
   - **Symmetric Harmonic Decomposition ($A_n, D_n$), Authority-Weighted PCA & `RRPP` Reflection Parity:** In standard EFA (Kuhl and Giardina 1982), four coefficients ($A_n, B_n, C_n, D_n$) are estimated per harmonic. Coefficients $A_n$ and $D_n$ encode the mathematically symmetric component of outline variation relative to the midrib, whereas $B_n$ and $C_n$ represent asymmetric components (fluctuating asymmetry, pressing distortions, mechanical shearing). Analyzing the symmetric harmonic component ($A_n, D_n$) via `Momocs::rm_asym(ef)` eliminates fluctuating asymmetry artifacts and guarantees parity between Tier 1 pristine and Tier 2 reflected leaves, verified in `03_fourier_extractor.R` via high-speed C-level randomized residual permutation PERMANOVA (`RRPP::lm.rrpp()`, Collyer & Adams 2018; with automatic fallback to `vegan::adonis2()`). Voucher metadata merge occurs **prior to PCA** so both `Sym_PC1`–`Sym_PC5` and primary `PC1`–`PC5` use authority- and quality-weighted covariance eigendecomposition (`stats::cov.wt(..., wt = tier_wt * leaf_quality_weight)`). Segment-level quality defenses compute bilateral asymmetry ratio (`pct_asymmetry`), harmonic roughness (`harmonic_roughness`, harmonics $n \ge 6$ vs. $n=1..3$), and gate outliers (`is_contour_outlier`: `pct_asymmetry > 0.22 | harmonic_roughness > 0.15`), exporting continuous `leaf_quality_weight` in `fourier_pcs.csv`.
3. **Tier 3 (Open-Outline Analysis):** Damaged rosettes lacking a full half-blade are evaluated using orthogonal polynomials (`Momocs::opoly`) and scalar caliper dimensions.
4. **Tier 4 (Holistic Rosette Deep Vision Embeddings):** Per-individual DBSCAN rosette patches (`data/cropped_patches/rosettes_dense/`), basal leaf patches (`basal_leaves_raw/`), and capitula (`capitula/`) are extracted; rosette patches are fed to DINOv2-ViT-B/14 to extract 768-dimensional latent representations capturing indumentum density and rosette architecture.

### Mathematical Alignment, Base-to-Apex Homologization & Within-Species Allometry
- **Base-to-Apex Polarity Disambiguation & Homologization (`norm = FALSE, start = FALSE`):** Rather than delegating outline normalization to `Momocs::efourier(..., norm = TRUE)` (which aligns the first harmonic ellipse major axis and induces $180^\circ$ polarity flips between obovate *P. anonyma* and ovate *P. dubia* leaves), `compute_geometric_metrics()` disambiguates `p_base` (petiole attachment) vs. `p_apex` (lamina tip) via terminal stalk width tapering across the terminal $5\%$–$15\%$ zones of the major axis (`w_span`) and spatial rosette centroid proximity (`rosette_centroid`). Contours are standardized to counter-clockwise winding, anchored with **Index 0 at `p_base` via `petiole_attachment_pt=p_base` across all `resample_normalized_contour()` calls** (Tier 1, folded, and Tier 2 reflected leaves), rotated so $\overrightarrow{p_{\text{base}} p_{\text{apex}}}$ lies along the $+X$ axis, and scaled **isotropically** by $\max(\text{span}_x, \text{span}_y)$ (preserving oriented blade aspect ratio `Momocs::coo_lw`). In `03_fourier_extractor.R`, outlines are centered and scaled (`coo_center %>% coo_scale`) and decomposed via **`Momocs::efourier(..., norm = FALSE, start = FALSE)`** to preserve true base-to-apex polarity.
- **Calibrated Millimeter Centroid Size, `RRPP` Allometric Trajectories & Authority-Weighted Pooled Within-Species Allometry:** Physical millimeter Centroid Size ($\text{CS}_{\text{mm}} = \text{CS}_{\text{px}} / \text{pixels\_per\_mm}$, filtered strictly by `is_calibrated_mm == TRUE`) is computed prior to unit-centroid-size scaling. In `04_gmm_morphotools.R`, `test_and_correct_allometry()` uses `RRPP::lm.rrpp()` and `RRPP::pairwise()` (Collyer & Adams 2018; with automatic fallback to `vegan::adonis2()`) for C-level randomized residual permutation testing of `Y ~ log_centsize * scientificName` on calibrated Tier 1 Gold anchors, exporting pairwise allometric trajectory angle ($\theta$) and magnitude comparisons to `outputs/reports/allometry_pairwise_trajectories.csv`. Size-dependent shape variation is then removed via **authority-weighted pooled within-species allometric regression** (`stats::lm(Y ~ log_centsize + scientificName, weights = authority_weight)`) (Klingenberg 2016), preventing interspecific size differences from being erased alongside ontogenetic scaling.
- **Composite `(catalogNumber, plant_individual_id)` Aggregation:** To avoid statistical pseudoreplication while preserving distinct individuals mounted on multi-plant herbarium sheets, leaf harmonic vectors are aggregated into median centroid profiles grouped per **`(catalogNumber, plant_individual_id)`** using within-plant quality weights (`leaf_quality_weight`), maintaining a $1.0\times$ voting weight per voucher collection event in CDA/GMM.
- **Empirical Gatekeeper Calibration:** Fold-detection chords and lyrate sinus depth thresholds are calibrated empirically against expert-labeled SAM 2 COCO annotations via `python main.py calibrate-geometry`.

## 3. Multi-Tiered Herbarium Misidentification Mitigation Architecture

Digital botanical aggregator audits reveal that 20% to 40% of public occurrences in the *P. dubia* complex are misidentified, nomenclaturally outdated, or ecologically misplaced. The pipeline implements six sequential mitigation tiers with a canonical authority weighting hierarchy (`authority_weight`: `Tier_1_Gold = 3.0`, `Tier_2_Silver = 1.5`, `Tier_3_Bronze = 0.5`) and Dual-Strategy Single-Taxon execution modes:

* **Tier 1 — Taxonomic Authority Stratification & Canonical Weighting (`3.0 / 1.5 / 0.5`):**
  - **Tier 1 (`Tier_1_Gold`, `authority_weight = 3.0`):** Nomenclatural types or determinations signed by recognized monographers (T.M. Barkley, D.K. Trock, R.R. Kowal, A.S. Weakley, J.F. Bain, A.M. Mahoney, J.B. Fuller).
  - **Tier 2 (`Tier_2_Silver`, `authority_weight = 1.5`):** Vouchers from established research herbaria (NCU, GA, US, NY, BRIT, MO, WIS, VDB, FLAS) with complete data.
  - **Tier 3 (`Tier_3_Bronze`, `authority_weight = 0.5`):** Unverified general floristic collections. Excluded from initial GMM/CDA parameterization and down-weighted (`0.5`) in weighted regressions and Random Forests.
  - **Dual-Strategy Single-Taxon Modes (`run_pipeline.sh`):** Supports **Strategy 1 (`--focal-taxon "<TAXON>" --gold-anchors 30`)**, which calibrates Steps 04–06 on all focal taxon vouchers plus $N$ `Tier_1_Gold` anchors per outgroup species before filtering Step 07 strictly to the focal taxon, and **Strategy 2 (`--focal-taxon "<TAXON>" --intraspecific-mode`)**, which runs strictly on a single species (`n_unique_taxa == 1`) with zero outgroup specimens, switching Steps 04–06 from `species_standardized` to label-blind GMM morphotypes (`Morphotype_K1..K_n` or geographic/edaphic ecotypes) to test for cryptic lineages and granite-outcrop ecotypes.
* **Tier 2 — Label-Blind Unsupervised Phenotypic Discovery on Gold/Silver Anchors (`clustvarsel`, `cov.rob` & `mclust`):**
  Symmetric EFA harmonics (`Sym_PC1`–`Sym_PC5`) are modeled without prior taxon labels. In `04_gmm_morphotools.R`, `run_gmm_cluster_analysis()` fits `clustvarsel::clustvarsel()` (Scrucca & Raftery 2018) and `mclust::Mclust()` exclusively on high-reliability `Tier_1_Gold` + `Tier_2_Silver` anchors (`!mahal_outliers` from `MASS::cov.rob`) with parsimonious covariance restriction (`EII, VII, EEI, VEI, EVI, VVI` when `n_samples / n_pcs < 5`), then projects `Tier_3_Bronze` vouchers onto `gmm_fit` via `predict(gmm_fit, newdata = X_pcs)` so noisy sheets do not bloat cluster covariances or inflate `gmm_uncertainty`.
* **Tier 3 — Passive Sample Projection & Mahalanobis Trimming in CDA (`MorphoTools2`):**
  In `run_canonical_discriminant_analysis()`, discriminant axes are trained on `Tier_1_Gold` ($w = 3.0$) and `Tier_2_Silver` ($w = 1.5$) anchors with per-taxon 95% chi-square Mahalanobis outlier trimming (`qchisq(0.95, df = p)`), uniform class priors (`rep(1/g, g)`), and honest Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (`loocv_accuracy`), while `Tier_3_Bronze` vouchers are projected passively. Exports `cda_predicted_taxon`, `cda_posterior_prob`, `cda_second_posterior_prob`, and `cda_posterior_margin`.
* **Tier 4 — Authority-Weighted Multi-Modal Consensus & Spatial Macroecology (`geodata`, `ranger`, `adespatial`, `blockCV`, `ecospat`):**
  `06_multimodal_spatial_rf.R` uses authority-weighted (`3.0 / 1.5 / 0.5`) circular phenology ($\sin / \cos \text{DOY}$) and edaphic envelopes in `evaluate_multimodal_concordance()`, centers Hopkins' Bioclimatic Law baseline at `pred_at_min_lat + 4.0 * (decimalLatitude - min_lat)`, passes `case.weights = case_wts` (`3.0 / 1.5 / 0.5`) into `ranger::ranger()` (Wright & Ziegler 2017), partitions variance via `vegan::varpart()`, evaluates spatially blocked 5-fold cross-validation accuracy via `blockCV::cv_spatial()` ($0.5^\circ \times 0.5^\circ$ grid blocks, exporting `spatial_block_cv_acc` alongside `OOB_Accuracy`; Valavi et al. 2019), and quantifies Schoener's $D$ and Warren's $I$ niche overlap in 2D kernel-smoothed `PCA-env` space via `ecospat` (Broennimann et al. 2012).
* **Tier 5 — Confident Learning, DINOv2 ViT Patch-Token Attribution & Batch-Effect Audits:**
  - **Confident Learning (`cleanlab`) & ViT Patch-Token Saliency:** `05_cleanlab_vision_xai.py` L2-normalizes 768-d DINOv2 embeddings prior to fold-internal `StandardScaler`, applies authority weights (`3.0 / 1.5 / 0.5`) combined with `class_weight="balanced"` in `LogisticRegression`, flags label errors ($C_{\text{error}} > 0.85$) via `cleanlab.filter.find_label_issues()`, exports `tomentum_taxon_discordance` alongside `is_label_corrupted` while controlling for foliar `surface_orientation` (`abaxial` vs. `adaxial`), and computes genuine **DINOv2 ViT final-layer patch-token saliency attribution** (`forward_features()["x_norm_patchtokens"]` projected onto classifier weights $\mathbf{w}_c$).
  - **Batch-Effect Controls:** Otsu mounting paper background neutralization masks sheet aging and paper stock color (`RGB(128, 128, 128)`) from DINOv2 vision encoders. Institutional one-way ANOVA audits verify $R^2 < 0.05$ across herbarium codes.
* **Tier 6 — Non-Georeferenced Voucher Coalescing (`98.4%` Completion) & Misidentification vs. Hybrid vs. Cryptic Disambiguation (`06`–`07`):**
  - **Non-Georeferenced Coalescing:** Step 07 (`07_triage_dashboard_synthesis.R`) enforces `validate_upstream_contracts()`, joins `multimodal_conflict_flags.csv` (georeferenced subset), and coalesces non-georeferenced vouchers directly from `morphometric_flags.csv` (Step 04) and `label_noise_audit.csv` (Step 05) using `unique(c("catalogNumber", ...))`, achieving **98.4% voucher completion (`5,117 / 5,201` vouchers in full production)** rather than dropping non-georeferenced herbarium sheets.
  - **Pure Misidentification vs. Hybrid Swarm:** When both Morphology (`cda_predicted_taxon`) and Vision (`vision_predicted_label`) agree on the same alternate species (`both_alt_agree`), or when either stream shows high-confidence conflict (`is_severe_misid`), Step 06 classifies the voucher as `"Severe_Triple_Stream_Conflict"` and Step 07 routes it to `Misidentification_Severe` $\rightarrow$ `Misidentification_Reassign` **before** evaluating `Hybrid_Intergrade_Swarm`.
  - **Reassign vs. Putative Cryptic Species (`is_crypt`):** Within `Misidentification_Severe`, ordinary misidentifications where both streams agree on a known alternate species or where either stream assigns high confidence to a known congener (`cda_posterior_prob >= 0.70` or `vision_c_error >= 0.85`) are classified as `Misidentification_Reassign`. `Putative_Cryptic_Species` is reserved strictly for true morphometric outliers (`morphometric_outlier_flag == TRUE`) lacking high-confidence known-taxon assignment that occupy peripheral GMM clusters (`gmm_cluster > 4` with $|\Delta\text{DOY}| \ge 14.0\text{ d}$) or exhibit extreme allochronic flowering divergence ($|\Delta\text{DOY}| \ge 21.0\text{ d}$ with `cda_posterior_prob < 0.55`).

## 4. Multi-Evidence Taxonomic Decision Matrix

| Taxonomic Status | Vegetative Morphometrics (Symmetric EFA / GMM) | Reproductive Macro-Morphology (Capitulum Aspect Ratio) | Cytology ($2n$ Cytotype; Kowal 1975) | Phylogenomics (nextRAD SNPs) | Micro-Edaphics & Niche (SSURGO / SoilGrids) | Actionable Taxonomic Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Distinct Species** | Distinct GMM cluster ($2\Delta\text{BIC} > 10$); LDA accuracy $\ge 90\%$ | Distinct capitulum height:width ratio ($p < 0.01$) | Fixed diploid ($2n = 44$ or $46$) or tetraploid ($2n = 88$) | Reciprocally monophyletic or diagnostic SNP cluster | Distinct edaphic zone / SSURGO map unit ($p < 0.01$; $D < D_{\text{null}}$) | Formal species-level recognition / resurrection under USC |
| **Subspecies / Variety** | Moderate LDA accuracy (75–89%); distinct Fourier outline mean | Conserved capitulum aspect ratio; slight size shift | Shared cytotype with nominal lineage ($2n = 44$ or $46$) | Clinal or regional genetic cluster with shallow divergence | Regional climatic or substrate differentiation ($D \approx 0.40 - 0.65$) | Varietal / subspecific circumscription (*P. dubia* var. *nov.*) |
| **Ecophenotypic Variant** | Continuous intergradation; single GMM component ($K=1$) | Overlapping capitulum morphology across habitats | Identical cytotype ($2n = 44$ or $46$) | Panmictic with parental populations; no lineage sorting | Continuous soil cline without sharp lithological break ($p > 0.05$) | Synonymy under polymorphic *Packera dubia* |
| **Hybrid Swarm / Reticulation** | Intermediately plotting passive samples; high entropy ($H \ge 0.50$) | High floral variation; irregular phyllary series | Aneuploid, triploid, or mixed tetraploid ($2n = 46, 68, 88$) | Admixed genomic ancestry; network reticulation | Restricted to ecotonal contact zones or disturbed ecotones | Designation as nothospecies (*Packera* $\times$*hybrid*) |
