## 1. Executive Summary and Theoretical Framework

I established this protocol to conduct automated high-throughput vegetative phenotyping and herbarium triage across North American ***Packera* Á. Löve & D. Löve** (Asteraceae: Senecioneae) with the taxonomically recalcitrant ***Packera dubia* complex** as my primary dissertation baseline. The lineage presents acute identification challenges on pressed sheets, as developmental heterophylly produces radical shape shifts between juvenile rosettes and mature cauline leaves, whereas manual curatorial annotations frequently confuse sympatric congeners.

I frame morphological diagnosability within the **Unified Species Concept (USC; de Queiroz 2007)** through **Integration by Congruence (Padial et al. 2010)**. Under the USC, species represent separately evolving metapopulation lineages. Lineage divergence generates morphological diagnosability and reproductive isolation alongside ecological divergence and reciprocal monophyly as contingent properties along the speciation continuum. I evaluate automated vegetative shape data alongside five independent lines of evolutionary and infrastructural evidence:

1. **Reproductive Macro-Morphology and Anthesis:** I quantify capitulum aspect ratios and phyllary counts alongside floret dimensions and 3-tier optical anthesis staging.
2. **Cytotaxonomic Cytotypes:** I harvest and cross-link public plus label-derived chromosome counts and C-value measurements ($2n = 44, 46, 88$; Kowal 1975) into Tier A SQLite tables (`cytological_records` paired with `taxon_cytology_summary`) so empirical base numbers and polyploid series test diploid versus tetraploid isolation without circular trait leakage.
3. **Reduced-Representation nextRAD Phylogenomics:** I align morphometric clusters against high-density SNP datasets that resolve reticulate evolution and hybrid swarms across lineage boundaries.
4. **0 to 15 cm Rhizosphere and USGS SGMC Bedrock Stack:** I cache edaphic profiles in SQLite `environmental_cache` and `environmental_rtree` through 0 to 15 cm depth-weighting (`(5 * v_0_5 + 10 * v_5_15) / 15`), where I combine SSURGO map units (`ssurgo_rock_outcrop_pct` and `ssurgo_bedrock_depth_cm`) and 30 m POLARIS grids alongside 100 m SOLUS100 layers, 6-class USGS SGMC lithology (`sgmc_lithology_class`), 10 m to 30 m USGS 3DEP topography, and Climatic ($D_{\text{clim}}$) versus Edaphic ($D_{\text{edaph}}$) niche equivalency tests (Warren et al. 2008).
5. **Central Relational WAL Architecture (`data/plantmatics.sqlite`):** All pipeline stages transact against a centralized SQLite WAL database (`PLANTMATICS_SCHEMA_USER_VERSION = 20261127`, aliased as `PACKERA_SCHEMA_USER_VERSION`, with automatic atomic zero-loss rename migration from legacy `data/packera_pipeline.sqlite` including `-wal` and `-shm` sidecars) housing Tier A shared tables (`taxon_synonyms`, `taxon_backbone_profiles`, `natureserve_subnational_ranges`, `vouchers`, `plant_individuals`, `extracted_leaves`, `voucher_reproductive_phenology`, `environmental_cache` with `environmental_rtree`, `genomic_samples`, `cytological_records`, plus `taxon_cytology_summary`) and Tier B run-scoped tables (`pipeline_runs`, `run_active_cohort`, `leaf_efa_harmonics`, `otu_morphometrics`, `spatial_rf_predictions`, `genetic_lineage_assignments`, `curation_triage`, plus `taxon_complexes`, with master view `v_integrative_triage_master`). Under schema version `20261127`, the database exposes dynamic Open-Set Recognition distances (`min_congener_mahal_d2`, `min_congener_taxon`, `open_set_novelty_p`), topological Euler Characteristic Transform descriptors (`ECT_PC1..PC3`, `Cauline_ECT_PC1..PC2`, `ect_lobe_complexity`, `cauline_ect_lobe_complexity`, `ect_bilateral_asym`, `heteroblasty_ect_distance`), Confident Learning noise flags (`is_confident_label_noise`), polyploid allele-balance cytotypes and `(h, Q12)` / `gdi` phylogenomic delimitation fields, public *Packera* nuclear backbone and organellar/`waxy` placement metrics (`public_plastome_match`, `public_its_match`, `waxy_lineage_match`, `public_backbone_clade`, `public_phytop_ils_index`, `public_phytop_ih_index`), CoL WCVP `2232` plus BHL API v3 and NatureServe Explorer taxonomic profiles (`backbone_basionym_name`, `protologue_url`, `natureserve_global_rank`), USACE NWPL wetland ratings (`nwpl_indicator_code`, `nwpl_wetland_score`), USGS `DS-801` soil geochemistry plus USFWS NWI metrics (`soil_ca_mg_molar_ratio`, `soil_ultramafic_ni_cr_index`, `soil_carbonate_c_pct`, `is_nwi_wetland`, `nwi_wetland_system`, `nwi_wetland_dist_m`), lineage population cohesion indicators (`cryptic_cluster_cohesion_pass`), four-tier unsequenced genomic propagation columns (`genomic_propagation_tier`, `tier0_calibrated_taxon`, `tier0_calibrated_prob`, `tier0_abstention_flag`, `deme_mahal_d2`, `cluster_propagated_gdi`), six specimen-linked cytology columns (`cytology_linkage_tier`, `voucher_sporophytic_2n`, `voucher_ploidy_level`, `taxon_reported_cytotypes`, `is_multi_cytotype_taxon`, `cytotype_reproductive_barrier_pass`), alongside three Step 05/07 hydro-edaphic and basionym guard columns (`hydro_edaphic_niche_regime`, `wetland_fidelity_concordance`, `basionym_synonymy_type`).

This multi-tiered relational design separates immutable raw observations from run-specific statistical cohorts, so I can test alternative taxonomic hypotheses without risking data contamination.

## 2. Phase 1 Protocol: Ingestion, Standardization and US EPA Level III Ecoregions (`01_voucher_harvester.py`)

I designed Phase 1 to solve the foundational challenge of herbarium data intake: raw specimen databases contain corrupted taxonomic names and collector duplicate conflicts alongside geographic clustering that distorts statistical models when records enter uncritically.

* **Darwin Core Schema Contract and Relational UPSERT (`vouchers`):**
  I harvest occurrences into SQLite `vouchers` (PK: `catalogNumber`) via atomic UPSERT while I resolve synonyms through `taxon_synonyms`, and I retain 100% of primary duplicate sheets plus verified nomenclatural types in Tier A with neutral `spatial_balance_weight = 1.0`. I export a synchronized CSV `data/tables/curated_vouchers.csv` with 25 core Darwin Core and authority columns (`catalogNumber`, `institutionCode`, `scientificName`, `species_raw`, `recordedBy`, `recordNumber`, `identifiedBy`, `determiner_tier`, `type_status`, `county`, `stateProvince`, `decimalLatitude`, `decimalLongitude`, `coordinateUncertainty`, `year`, `month`, `day`, `eventDate`, `regional_group`, `image_path`, `exsiccatae_key`, `is_primary_duplicate`, `duplicate_count`, `species_standardized`, and `spatial_balance_weight`), whereas non-mutating duplicate conflict indicators (`exsiccatae_taxonomic_conflict` and `exsiccatae_gold_taxon`) persist directly in SQLite `vouchers`.
* **Herbarium Rooftop and County-Centroid Coordinate Quarantine (`scripts/core/coord_hygiene_utils.py`):**
  During Step 01 ingestion, I classify suspect coordinates into `is_centroid_or_inst_coord` paired with `coord_quarantine_reason` rather than dropping voucher records from morphological or taxonomic curation. Records matching known herbarium institutional coordinates (such as UNC Coker Hall rooftops when paired with municipal locality strings) receive `coord_quarantine_reason = "herbarium_institution_rooftop"`, while records matching US county centroids from `us_spatial_graph` receive `coord_quarantine_reason = "county_centroid_suspect"`. Wild microhabitat locality phrases alongside `is_type_specimen` records remain unflagged so real bedrock outcrops and primary nomenclatural types stay active, and all quarantined vouchers retain `georef_qc_status = "valid"` plus their raw `decimalLatitude`/`decimalLongitude` coordinates across SQLite `vouchers` and `v_integrative_triage_master`. Downstream spatial kernel density weighting in Steps 03 to 05 and Tier B preflight (`compute_spatial_balance_weights()` and `spatially_balance_and_thin_vouchers()`) excludes `is_centroid_or_inst_coord` and unpromoted `Tier_3_Bronze` rows from local density `d_local` while recomputing active anchor weights.
* **Dynamic Taxon Standardization, WCVP `2232` Backbone, and Range Screening:**
  I configure genus and family scope alongside `taxa.synonym_genera`, `taxa.eastern_resident_whitelist`, and `taxa.transcontinental_boreal_taxa` dynamically in `config/config.yaml` and resolve them through `scripts/core/step01_biogeography_utils.py`. I standardize binomials and basionyms dynamically through `expand_taxon_synonyms_gbif()`, paired with `scripts/data_prep/download_taxonomic_backbone.py`, which harvests Catalogue of Life WCVP dataset `2232` accepted names alongside homotypic versus heterotypic synonym arrays, Biodiversity Heritage Library (BHL) API v3 protologue deep-links, and NatureServe Explorer global and subnational ranks into flat `data/taxonomy/` CSVs plus SQLite `taxon_backbone_profiles` and `natureserve_subnational_ranges`. When I omit taxon flags, the harvester queries all species in the active genus through `discover_genus_packera_species_gbif()`, whereas `resolve_eastern_resident_whitelist()` unions YAML residents with native TDWG Level 3 and NatureServe taxa in SQLite (`normalize_packera_orthography()` normalizes `"Packera millefolia"` and `"Packera ×memmingeri"` cleanly), and `filter_vouchers_by_usda_county_range()` falls back automatically to SQLite `natureserve_subnational_ranges` for unmapped post-2000 segregates or Canadian provinces (`natureserve_state_in_range`, `natureserve_exotic_or_waif`, `out_of_range`). When I pass `taxon` or `focal_taxon` CLI flags, the harvester restricts analysis strictly to those specified taxa, and preflight reconciles any requested-taxon vouchers stored in SQLite `vouchers` into the active run cohort via `reconcile_curated_vouchers_csv_with_db()`.
* **Two-Pass `StillImage` Pagination and Ecoregion-Bounded Geographic Scope:**
  I query GBIF with `mediaType="StillImage"` across configured `countries` (default `["US", "CA"]`) using a two-pass country quota allocator: Pass 1 grants each country up to `ceil(max_records_per_taxon / len(countries))` records so US occurrences never starve Canadian collections, and Pass 2 rolls unused capacity back into non-exhausted countries from saved offsets. Under `geographic_scope = "eastern_na"`, I filter Western US states and Western or Northern Canadian provinces (`BC, AB, SK, YT, NT, NU`) plus longitudes west of `-100.0` deg W alongside a `typeStatus="*"` rescue query per country, whereas `evaluate_transcontinental_ecoregion_gate()` screens transcontinental boreal taxa (`Packera paupercula`, `Packera indecora`, `Packera pauciflora`) by EPA and CEC Level III ecoregion so boreal forest extensions (such as `Boreal_Shield` and `Northern_Lakes_and_Forests`) pass while Great Plains and Cordilleran ecoregions drop.
* **Indumentum-Safe Optical Sharpness QC, Authority-First Deduplication, and WebP Storage:**
  When `check_sharpness: true` is active in `config/config.yaml`, `validate_image_quality()` measures both global central-ROI Laplacian variance and the 90th-percentile Laplacian variance across an `8 x 8` non-overlapping tile grid (`sharpness_score = max(global_lap_var, tile_p90_lap_var)` against `min_sharpness_laplacian: 80.0`), so crisp label or margin detail on densely tomentose leaves passes while blurred or upscaled scans reject as `"blurry_or_upscaled"`. I rank duplicate gatherings by verified type status, in-range standing, `determiner_tier` (`Tier_1_Gold` ahead of `home_herbarium`), `home_herbarium`, and scan resolution, and I record `exsiccatae_taxonomic_conflict` alongside `exsiccatae_gold_taxon` without mutating conflicting sheet labels. Full-sheet imagery in `data/raw_vouchers/` and crops in `data/cropped_patches/` default to quality-92 `.webp` compression (`method=4` via PIL), which yields a 70% to 80% disk reduction relative to JPEG or PNG with zero edge degradation.
* **Official US EPA Level III Ecoregion Point-in-Polygon Delineation:**
  I assign `regional_group` strictly through spatial intersection against official **US EPA Level III Ecoregions** (Omernik & Griffith 2014; `data/environmental/epa_ecoregions_l3/`):
  $$\text{regional\_group}_i = \text{Ecoregion}_k \iff (x_i, y_i) \in \mathcal{P}_k$$
* **Ungeoreferenced Specimen Fallback Policy:**
  When coordinate data are missing ($y_i = \text{NA}$), I evaluate the US Census county boundary polygon $\mathcal{C}_i$:
  $$\text{regional\_group}_i = \begin{cases} \text{Ecoregion}_k & \text{if } \frac{\text{Area}(\mathcal{C}_i \cap \mathcal{P}_k)}{\text{Area}(\mathcal{C}_i)} \ge 0.95 \\ \text{NA} & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$
* **Nomenclatural Type Hierarchy and Priority Engine:**
  - `PRIMARY_TYPE_STATUSES = {"HOLOTYPE", "LECTOTYPE", "NEOTYPE", "EPITYPE", "ISOTYPE", "ISOLECTOTYPE", "ISONEOTYPE"}` (`Tier_0_Primary_Type`, rank 2).
  - `SECONDARY_TYPE_STATUSES = {"SYNTYPE", "ISOSYNTYPE", "PARATYPE", "ISOPARATYPE", "TYPE", "COTYPE"}` (`Tier_0_Secondary_Type`, rank 1).
  - I resolve fine-grained ICN priority through `classify_nomenclatural_type_subrank()` (`2.2` primary, `2.0` isotype, `1.5` syntype, `1.0` paratype; Turland et al. 2018). I exempt verified types from uncertainty or partial-date filters and retain them unconditionally during spatial thinning and outgroup selection.
* **2-Layer Dynamic Authority Stratification (`parse_determiner_tier` and `apply_corpus_authority_tiers`):**
  - **Layer 1 (`Tier_1_Gold` Monographers, Types, and Determiner Isolation):** Dynamic authority discovery (`discover_corpus_authorities`) evaluates `identifiedBy` independently of `recordedBy`, and it falls back to `recordedBy` only when `identifiedBy` is blank or explicitly references the collector, which prevents collector-as-determiner false Gold promotions. I block non-person database adjustment strings (`"Nomenclatural Adjustment"` and `"Nomenclature update"` alongside `"Taxonomic update"` and `"Annotation"`). Non-type `Tier_1_Gold` status requires an initial-qualified or curated North American *Packera* and Senecioneae specialist entry (`PACKERA_SPECIALISTS` and `SPECIALIST_PATTERNS`: Barkley, Trock, Kowal, Weakley, Bain, Mahoney, Freeman, Cronquist, Greenman, Weber, Löve, Spellenberg, Pippen, Uttal, Fernald, Rydberg, Heller, Kral, Godfrey, Sorrie, Nesom, Strother, Turner, Golden, Greene, Moore, Boufford, alongside Britton), whereas bare common surnames (`"fuller"` included) and non-type historical protologue author matches stay excluded from `Tier_1_Gold` (Goodwin et al. 2015; Maldonado et al. 2015).
  - **Layer 2 (`Tier_2_Silver` Corpus Determiners, Regional Herbaria, Investigator Guards, and Batch Demotion):** Determiners discovered dynamically by `discover_corpus_authorities()` ($\ge 5$ sheets across $\ge 3$ herbaria with $\le 15\%$ duplicate conflict), investigator determinations (`J. Brandon Fuller` / `B. Fuller` at qualified herbaria with rich locality), and curated regional herbaria (`MAJOR_HERBARIA_CODES` plus `dynamic_herbaria`) cap strictly at `Tier_2_Silver`. In `apply_corpus_authority_tiers()`, I protect multi-herbarium and multi-species monographer loans by preserving `Tier_1_Gold` when a curated specialist annotates across multiple species or institutions even when `dateIdentified` is unrecorded, whereas undated single-herbarium, single-species batch transfers ($\ge 25$ sheets across $\ge 10$ historical collectors with $\ge 85\%$ concentrated in one species where `recordedBy != identifiedBy`) demote from `Tier_1_Gold` to `Tier_2_Silver` for out-of-sample screening. Personal field collections (`recordedBy == identifiedBy`) and explicitly dated specialist annotations remain `Tier_1_Gold`, whereas unverified determinations route to `Tier_3_Bronze`.
* **Preflight Authority Refresh and Congeneric Neighbor Filtering (`run_pipeline.sh`):**
  During preflight, the orchestrator re-applies `apply_corpus_authority_tiers()` and filters `auto_neighbors` candidates against `^Packera\s+[a-z]{3,}$`, so `outgroup_taxa` always retains valid congeneric binomials and excludes malformed family tokens.

Now that Phase 1 establishes an authoritative, georeferenced specimen cohort, I feed these vetted vouchers directly into the vision pipeline.

## 3. Phase 2 Protocol: Pure LeafMachine3 ONNX Pipeline and Botanical Gatekeeping (`02_segment_and_extract.py`)

Herbarium sheets present challenging visual artifacts: white mounting tape and fragment packets alongside discolored paper and overlapping stems frequently cause standard bounding-box detectors to fail. I address this challenge by deploying **LeafMachine3** (`lm3`) ONNX Runtime models alongside botanical gatekeeper algorithms.

### 3.1 LeafMachine3 Inference Stack (`scripts/vision/`)
* **Scale Calibration (Modules 1, 6 and 7):** Module 1 (`lm3_mp_conversion_factor__sqrt_fit`, $R^2 = 0.905$, MAE 2.3%) predicts resolution priors; Module 6 ensemble detects rulers via `lm3_ruler_classifier`; Module 7 (`lm3_ruler_segmenter` with deterministic 1D FFT and autocorrelation `solve_ruler_tick_lattice_px_per_mm` fallback alongside FieldPrism markers) establishes physical scale (`pixels_per_mm`, `is_calibrated_mm`).
* **Stage 1 Plant Detection, Dissected PCD Settings and Asymmetric Nested-Box Deduplication (`lm3_plant_detector__yolo26x_det_1280`):** I ingest herbarium sheets into a `max_working_dim = 3200` working frame (`cv2.INTER_AREA`) and execute Stage 1 plant and organ detection at $1280 \times 1280$ resolution (`conf = 0.25`, `iou = 0.55`, `include_partial = true`) with dynamic rectangular stride-32 letterboxing (`ultra_rep.letterbox(auto=True, stride=32)`, `cv2.INTER_LINEAR_EXACT`) alongside float32 reciprocal scaling (`*= float32(1.0) / float32(255.0)`). Because deeply pinnatisect and bipinnatisect blades across North American *Packera* congeners are frequently scored by Stage 1 as `Leaf_PARTIAL` (`class_id == 1`) or receive a high-confidence sub-box on a terminal lobe nested inside a lower-confidence parent whole-leaf bounding box, I retain both `Leaf_WHOLE` (`class_id == 0`) and `Leaf_PARTIAL` (`class_id == 1`) candidates (`segment_partial_leaves = true`) and apply parent-preserving asymmetric nested-box deduplication (`overlap = 0.95`). When a smaller leaf box is nested inside an enclosing parent leaf box ($\text{area}_{\text{parent}} \ge 1.35 \cdot \text{area}_{\text{child}}$ and $\text{intersection} / \text{area}_{\text{child}} \ge 0.90$), I retain the enclosing parent full-leaf bounding box and suppress the nested sub-lobe box.
* **Whole-Sheet Specimen Segmentation and CIE $L^*a^*b^*$ Paper Cleaning (`lm3_specimen_segmenter`):** Module 4 (`Specimen_Segmenter` `UNet++ v9`, $768 \times 768$) generates 4-class whole-sheet probability maps (`bg` and `plant_main` alongside `text_bars` and `paper`). I apply `paperclean_llr_hyst_keepblobs` (`LLR_Hyst_KeepBlobs`) log-likelihood hysteresis in CIE $L^*a^*b^*$ color space at `max_working_dim = 3200` strictly at the whole-sheet level to isolate the herbarium mounting sheet (`specimen_paper_cleaned_mask`) and plant foreground (`plant_binary_mask`), which removes mounting-paper pockets inside deep leaf sinuses while it preserves detached pinnae.
* **Stage 2 Unfilled Lamina Segmentation, Paper-Pocket Cleanup, and Fold/Damage QC Gating (`lm3_leaf_segmenter__yolo26x_seg_1024`):** I extract tight `crop_pad = 0.0` bounding-box crops from Stage 1 detections (`conf = 0.35`, `segment_partial_leaves = false` so `Leaf_PARTIAL` fragments enter only as zero-whole-leaf rescues flagged `LEAF_PARTIAL_FRAGMENT`) at $1024 \times 1024$ resolution (`conf = 0.35`) with dynamic rectangular stride-32 letterboxing (`auto=True, stride=32`), `Leaf_Segmenter` `retina_masks: true`, and `report.masks.subtract_holes: true`. Because touching lobes on deeply dissected *Packera* blades enclose white mounting-paper spaces where `Leaf_Segmenter` `Hole` recall drops (`mAP50 = 0.429`), I omit outer-polygon hole flood-filling on `Leaf` (`class_id == 0`) masks so native `0`-pixel sinus voids stay open, and I subtract trapped interior paper pockets via `LM3SpecimenSegmenter` (`specimen_paper_cleaned_mask`) paired with guarded bimodal CIE $L^*a^*b^*$ background screening. During contour extraction (`open_hairline_sinus_bridges_and_measure_voids`), I open hairline lobe-tip bridges (`<= 3.5` px) so single-loop contours trace true sinuses, whereas broadly overlapping lobe loops (`enclosed_void_frac > 0.020`) are flagged `ENCLOSED_INTERLOBE_VOID` to withhold distorted single-loop EFA harmonics while retaining hole-preserved $32 \times 360$ `RETR_CCOMP` ECT descriptors and void-aware solidity. In `evaluate_leaf_damage_and_fold_qc()` and `evaluate_step03_lm3_efa_qc()`, I screen out border-truncated blades (`CROP_BORDER_CLIPPED`), severe insect holes (`hole_area_frac > 0.035`), bent or dog-eared midveins (`FOLDED_MIDVEIN_TRACE`, `APICAL_TIP_FOLD`), longitudinal half-folds (`LONGITUDINAL_MARGIN_CREASE`), and unilateral tears (`UNILATERAL_ASYMMETRY_OR_TEAR`), granting dissected Dice tolerance (`bilateral_dice >= 0.58`, `|si_a| < 0.28`) strictly when `bilateral_lobe_symmetry` verifies multi-lobe inflections on both margins.
* **31-Keypoint Anatomical Pose Estimation and Petiole/Landmark Delegation (`lm3_landmark_detector__yolo26x_pose_640`):** I pad each leaf crop with a 10% white border (`WHITE_PAD_FRAC = 0.10`, RGB `[255, 255, 255]`) and apply dynamic rectangular stride-32 letterboxing (`auto=True, stride=32`) at $640 \times 640$ resolution. I predict 31 anatomical keypoints (`mid15_pet5`) and normalize 1-based canonical keys (`midvein_1..15`, `petiole_1..5`, `petiole_6`) to distinct 0-based native `LeafMachine3` `KPT_NAMES` (`midvein_0..14`, `petiole_0..4`, `petiole_tip`) without key collisions. I delegate petiole skeleton geometry and landmark caliper widths directly to `leafmachine3.core.petiole.measure_petiole` and `leafmachine3.core.landmark_metrics.compute_measurements`.
* **Radial-and-Angular Euler Characteristic Transform (ECT) and Product Rendering:** I delegate $32 \times 360$ (`ect_num_dirs = 360, num_thresh = 32`) radial-and-angular Euler Characteristic Transform computation and visual product rendering directly to `leafmachine3.core.ect_compute.compute_ect` and `leafmachine3.reporting.ect_products.ect_product_for` alongside `leafmachine3.reporting.leaf_products.render_leaf_products`, which saves `.npy` matrices to `data/ect/*_ect.npy` and records `ect_path` in `extracted_leaves`.

### 3.2 Read-Only LeafMachine3 Ingestion, Organ Indexing and Scale Calibration (`scripts/pipeline/02_segment_and_extract.py`)
* **DBSCAN Plant Decomposition (`cluster_voucher_plants_dbscan`):** I cluster organs into individual plants (`plant_individual_id = 0, 1, ...`) and estimate rosette centroids alongside crown extents.
* **Rosette Caudex Crown Estimation (`estimate_rosette_crown_point`):** I identify the root-shoot transition zone ($p_{\text{crown}}$). I measure petiole length as outer-rosette geodesic distance:
  $$L_{\text{petiole}} = \max\left(d_{\text{geodesic}}(p_{\text{base}}, p_{\text{crown}}),\, \|p_{\text{base}} - p_{\text{crown}}\|_2\right)$$
  aggregated at $P_{75}$ across outer basal leaves. I evaluate scape height ($H_{\text{plant}}$) across both straight and bent stems:
  $$H_{\text{plant}} = \|p_{\text{bend}} - p_{\text{crown}}\|_2 + \|p_{\text{corymb}} - p_{\text{bend}}\|_2$$
* **Topological and Heteroblastic Anatomical `leaf_zone` Classification (`classify_leaf_zone_anatomical`) and Fast CPU Re-Scoring (`rescore_step02_qc_and_zones.py`):** Because acropetal heteroblasty between petiolate basal rosette leaves and subpetiolate or sessile cauline stem leaves drives morphological contrast across North American *Packera* (Barkley 1962; Trock 2006), `classify_leaf_zone_anatomical()` in `scripts/vision/organ_crown_metrics.py` resolves shoot zones polarity-invariantly across four organ-presence regimes: (1) `Roots` and `Capitula` jointly present, (2) `Roots` present alone (with proximal petiolate leaves anchoring a caudex box when bare roots are sparse), (3) `Capitula`-only flowering stalks applying the `Case 3B` clipped aerial stem veto (`raw_d_crown > 1.35 * r_hub` when stem fraction `s_prelim > 0.28`, or `> 0.18` near capitula), and (4) organless `Case 4A`/`4B` rosette mounts. Long-petiole flowering rosette leaves (`petiole_to_blade_ratio >= 0.35` and distal stem fraction `s_stem_tip <= 0.32`) rescue intact basal leaves near the root crown, whereas sessile narrow blades along elevated aerial nodes override cleanly to `cauline`. The classifier outputs continuous basal probabilities `p_basal` (`leaf_zone = "basal"` when `p_basal >= 0.50`, else `"cauline"`) alongside `leaf_zone_confidence = round(max(p_basal, 1.0 - p_basal), 4)` and `dist_from_crown_norm`. To recalibrate leaf zones, hairline sinus bridges (`open_hairline_sinus_bridges_and_measure_voids`), void-aware `efa_qc_pass` flags, and margin tick-lattice ruler scales (`solve_ruler_tick_lattice_px_per_mm`) across already segmented sheets without repeating GPU inference, I run `scripts/vision/rescore_step02_qc_and_zones.py`, which updates Tier A SQLite tables and companion CSVs atomically on CPU.
* **Read-Only LeafMachine3 Persistence and Downstream Step 03 QC (`evaluate_step03_lm3_efa_qc`):** Step 02 operates as a read-only `LeafMachine3` ingestion adapter that persists 100% of segmented leaf instances, native Voronoi bilateral symmetry metrics (`bilateral_dice`, `si_a`), void-aware QC indicators (`efa_qc_pass`, `qc_flag_reason`), 120-vertex tip-up `lamina_base`-anchored contours (`contour_xy_json`), and $32 \times 360$ ECT arrays (`data/ect/*.npy`) directly to SQLite `extracted_leaves`. Downstream clump and fragment exclusion executes deterministically inside Step 03 (`evaluate_step03_lm3_efa_qc`), so statistical QC thresholds can be audited or recalibrated across Tier B runs without repeating GPU segmentation.
* **Dissected-Leaf Rachis Bridging (`_build_native_lm3_crop_masks`):** In deeply pinnatifid leaves with narrow segmentation breaks, I retain secondary `Leaf` components (`>= 64` px) inside a crop only when they are topologically bridged to the primary focal lamina through the `Petiole`/rachis mask (`class_id == 1`) alongside `specimen_paper_cleaned_mask`. This topological constraint preserves pinnatisect lobes while preventing adjacent overlapping leaves at crop borders from fusing into a single contour.
* **300-DPI PNG Organ Montages and Native Visual Caches (`scripts/vision/step02_figures.py`):** Step 02 exports downstream-QC-filtered 300-DPI PNG plates (`fig02a_lm3_organ_and_ect_exemplars.png` and `fig02b_segmentation_qc_and_phenophase.png`), unlimited taxon-indexed `LeafMachine3` cutouts and polar ECT badges (`lm3_assets/` and `ect_overlays/`), and capped whole-sheet diagnostic overlays (`lm3_sheet_overlays/`, `max_sheets = 24`).
* **Non-Destructive Run Purging (`purge_runs_only`) vs. Full Segment Reset (`purge_segments_and_runs`):** To test downstream statistical or ecological hypotheses without wasting hours of GPU segmentation, `./run_pipeline.sh --purge-runs-only` (`python main.py purge-runs-only`) calls `purge_runs_only()` in `scripts/core/db.py` to remove `runs/`, `outputs/`, Step 03 to 07 CSVs, and Tier B SQLite tables while preserving 100% of Step 01 vouchers and Step 02 masks, contours, ECT arrays, and Tier A SQLite tables (resetting `min_bedrock_depth_buffer_cm` to `NULL` so buffered lithic extractions refresh cleanly on the next run). Conversely, `./run_pipeline.sh --purge-segments` (`python main.py purge-segments`) invokes `purge_segments_and_runs()` to wipe Step 02 to 07 masks, contours, ECT arrays, organ crops, `runs/`, and SQLite Step 02 to 07 rows followed by `VACUUM`, while leaving Step 01 `vouchers`, `taxon_synonyms`, `environmental_cache`, `data/raw_vouchers/`, and `data/tables/curated_vouchers.csv` untouched.

By partitioning basal rosette and cauline stem leaves into explicit anatomical zones while I reject severed fragments and mounting tape artifacts, I preserve developmental homology across both foliar series.

## 4. Phase 3 and 4 Protocol: Morphometrics, EFA, Topological ECT, Allometry and Ordination (`03` and `04`)

In Phase 3 and Phase 4, I transform discrete leaf outlines into continuous mathematical and topological functions. At first glance, elliptic Fourier analysis and Euler Characteristic Transform topology seem different, but that difference is superficial because both approaches quantify boundary and sinus variance relative to biological homology.

### 4.1 Homologized Closed-Contour EFA, $32 \times 360$ ECT Decomposition and 3-Way Confidence Routing (`03_fourier_extractor.R`, `step03_config_scale_utils.R`, `dissected_leaf_utils.R`, and `fourier_parallel_utils.R`)
* **Closed-Contour QC Gating (`evaluate_step03_lm3_efa_qc`), Shape-Free Zone Routing & `<= 35 mm` Proximity Consolidation (`classify_and_partition_leaf_zones`, `consolidate_fragmented_sheet_individuals`, `apply_otu_confidence_routing_tier`):**
  I restrict `Momocs::efourier` strictly to intact closed contours passing `evaluate_step03_lm3_efa_qc()` and `efa_qc_pass == 1`, so I withhold damaged, border-clipped, folded, and unresolved clump masks while retaining hole-preserved $32 \times 360$ ECT arrays for deeply incised multi-component blades. Next, `classify_and_partition_leaf_zones()` partitions passing leaves across three confidence-gated shoot zones strictly from crown distance (`dist_from_crown_norm`) and plant-box vertical position (`cy_rel_plant_bbox` / `cy_rel_sheet`) with zero `petiole_to_blade_ratio` or blade-area circularity: `basal` (`leaf_zone == "basal"` with `leaf_zone_confidence >= 0.65`), `ambiguous_transitional` (putative basal blades below the `0.65` confidence gate), and `cauline` (for the orthogonal stem-leaf and heteroblasty track, where the function rescues lower-confidence blades only when no `>= 0.65` basal leaf survives and remaining leaves are not all crown-verified cauline). Before OTU aggregation, `consolidate_fragmented_sheet_individuals()` merges isolated cauline or single-leaf fragments onto the primary basal rosette only when physical distance stays within `<= 35.0 mm` (`<= 0.12` normalized sheet span when uncalibrated), re-zoning upper-stem strays strictly by position. During individual OTU assembly, `apply_otu_confidence_routing_tier()` sets `morph_tier_source = "Transitional_Passive"` when an individual relies on `< 0.65` transitional leaves and `"Cauline_Only_Fallback"` when zero basal blades remain. Both `Transitional_Passive` and `Cauline_Only_Fallback` individuals receive `is_passive = TRUE` and `authority_weight = 0.0` (unless pinned as a verified nomenclatural type), so Step 03 and Step 04 strictly isolate transitional lower-stem blades and cauline fallback sheets from primary `Basal_Rosette` reference covariance matrices, `residualize_ontogenetic_phenophase_plasticity()`, and `audit_institutional_batch_effects()`. All individual leaf vectors and zone classifications persist to `leaf_efa_harmonics` alongside `leaf_efa_harmonics_individual.csv`.
* **Scale Provenance Gating (`resolve_step03_calibration_provenance`) & Per-Leaf Tip-Up Homology:**
  `resolve_step03_calibration_provenance()` inside `step03_config_scale_utils.R` alongside `fetch_step03_contours_from_db()` in `db_utils.R` marks `mp_estimated`, `predicted_from_megapixels`, `tick_lattice_unboxed`, and `uncalibrated` leaves as `is_calibrated_mm = FALSE`, preserves `tick_lattice_unboxed` verbatim in `scale_source`, and sets `centroid_size`, `log_centsize`, `area_mm2`, `petiole_length_mm`, `petiole_width_mm`, `blade_length_mm`, `blade_width_mm`, and `cauline_blade_length_mm` to `NA` on both individual and OTU outputs while preserving dimensionless `petiole_to_blade_ratio`. `fetch_step03_contours_from_db()` resolves `mask_is_upright` individually per leaf from each contour's `lm3_from_tipup` attribute rather than broadcasting across a batch, and `discover_step03_photometry_cols()` propagates `blade_cie_l_norm`, `blade_cie_l_sd`, `blade_cie_l_sheet_delta`, `blade_cie_a_delta`, and `blade_cie_b_delta` through SQLite and unweighted basal OTU medians.
* **Landmark-Anchored Tip-Up Homology and Harmonic Power Calibration:**
  I center and isotropically scale `lamina_base -> lamina_tip` homologized contours via `Momocs::coo_center() %>% Momocs::coo_scale()` (with horizontal major-axis alignment `align_contour_horizontal_xax` prior to `rm_asym`), which preserves true length-to-width aspect ratios below $1.0$ on reniform and cordate blades. Harmonic power is defined via `load_step03_fourier_config()` YAML/CLI bounds as:
  $$\text{Power}_n = \frac{A_n^2 + B_n^2 + C_n^2 + D_n^2}{2},\quad \frac{\sum_{n=1}^{H_{\text{opt}}} \text{Power}_n}{\sum_{n=1}^{H_{\max}} \text{Power}_n} \ge 0.99$$
  I dynamically determine the harmonic count $H_{\text{opt}} \in [6, 24]$ that captures $\ge 99\%$ cumulative power, and I execute `Momocs::efourier(out_obj, nb.h = H_opt, norm = FALSE, start = FALSE)`.
* **Uniform Bilateral Symmetrization (Claude 2008):**
  $$A_n^{\text{sym}} = A_n,\quad D_n^{\text{sym}} = D_n,\quad B_n^{\text{sym}} = 0,\quad C_n^{\text{sym}} = 0,\quad \text{pct\_asymmetry} = \frac{\sum_{n=1}^{H_{\text{opt}}} (B_n^2 + C_n^2)}{\sum_{n=1}^{H_{\text{opt}}} (A_n^2 + B_n^2 + C_n^2 + D_n^2)}$$
* **Topological Euler Characteristic Transform (ECT) Alignment, Symmetrization and SVD (`compute_leaf_ect_features`):**
  Because Fourier harmonics can smooth deep pinnatisect sinuses and fail on multi-island compound blades, `compute_leaf_ect_features()` in `scripts/morphometrics/dissected_leaf_utils.R` loads each leaf's $32 \times 360$ radial-and-angular ECT matrix from `data/ect/*_ect.npy` (or computes the exact EmbeddedGraph cycle Euler characteristic $\chi(\phi_j, t_i) = \#V(h \le t_i) - \#E(\max(h_u, h_v) \le t_i)$ across 360 angular directions and 32 radial thresholds via `compute_cycle_ect_r()`; Ayub et al. 2026; Weaver 2026). Column 1 is anchored to the leaf apex ($\phi = 0$), and directional columns $2..\text{nc}$ are reflected to form the bilateral symmetric half-matrix $\mathbf{E}_{\text{sym}} = \frac{1}{2}(\mathbf{E} + \mathbf{E}_{\text{refl}})$ under a signed log-modulus transform $\operatorname{sign}(\mathbf{E}_{\text{sym}})\log(1 + |\mathbf{E}_{\text{sym}}|)$. From each matrix, I compute bilateral ECT asymmetry (`ect_bilateral_asym`) and topological lobe complexity (`ect_lobe_complexity` and `cauline_ect_lobe_complexity`) as the sum of excess directional total variation $\operatorname{mean}_j(\max(0, (\text{TV}_j - 2)/2))$ and multi-component sinus excess $\operatorname{mean}(\max(0, \chi - 1))$. I then extract authority-weighted SVD principal components anchored on active basal leaves (`ECT_PC1..PC3`, positively aligned with `ect_lobe_complexity`) alongside orthogonal cauline ECT components (`Cauline_ECT_PC1..PC2`) and within-plant basal-to-cauline topological divergence (`heteroblasty_ect_distance`).
* **Type Outlier Immunity, Single OTU-Anchored PCA, Pure Basal Primary OTU Aggregation and Orthogonal Cauline Track:**
  I exempt intact type contours from within-taxon Mahalanobis and MCD outlier screening (`is_contour_outlier[is_type_specimen] <- FALSE`). To eliminate multi-leaf pseudoreplication in ordination space, Step 03 fits one authoritative weighted PCA (`stats::cov.wt()`) strictly at the `Basal_Rosette` OTU level across active anchors (`authority_weight * spatial_balance_weight`), propagates OTU authority status onto leaves (`0.0` passive weight for non-basal or outlier non-type leaves), projects both OTUs and leaves into that shared space, and screens leaf PCs via `screen_regime_stratified_mcd_outliers()` with automatic OTU re-aggregation whenever a multi-leaf rosette sheds an outlier leaf. I export PCs accounting for $\ge 95\%$ variance ($k_{\text{export}} = \max(5, k_{95})$, bounded by YAML `min_export_pcs` and `max_export_pcs`). During coordinate-wise OTU aggregation to `(catalogNumber, plant_individual_id)` via `aggregate_step03_otu_medians_fast()` (`reconcile_step03_basal_otu_medians()` delegates to the same helper), I compute unweighted primary OTU medians (`A1..D_H`, `PC1..PCk`, `ECT_PC1..PC3`, `ect_lobe_complexity`, `solidity`, `dissection_index`, and all discovered `blade_cie_*` photometry columns) strictly across clean `leaf_zone == 'basal'` leaves whenever $\ge 1$ basal leaf is present on the specimen (falling back to cauline medians as `Cauline_Only_Fallback` only when a specimen has 0 basal leaves), so cauline bracts never contaminate the basal rosette outline. Simultaneously, I aggregate `leaf_zone == 'cauline'` leaves into an orthogonal cauline and heteroblasty track (`cauline_leaf_count`, `cauline_solidity_med`, `cauline_dissection_index_med`, `cauline_aspect_ratio_med`, `cauline_blade_length_mm` on calibrated rows, `cauline_ect_lobe_complexity`, `basal_vs_cauline_length_ratio`, `basal_vs_cauline_solidity_delta`, `heteroblasty_efa_distance`, `heteroblasty_ect_distance`, `Cauline_PC1..PC3`, and `Cauline_ECT_PC1..PC2`).

### 4.2 Determiner Authority Weighting, Spatial Population Demes and Reference Anchors (`authority_weighting_utils.R`, `plasticity_mitigation_utils.R`)
* **Hierarchical Heteroblasty Partitioning, 10 km Spatial Demes and Plasticity Mitigation:**
  To account for within-plant heteroblasty and local microsite plasticity without pre-shrinking feature coordinates before cross-validation or MANOVA, I separate variance sources across three explicit stages:
  - **Level 1 (Orthogonal Basal and Cauline Heteroblasty Partitioning):** Primary OTU coordinates represent unweighted basal rosette medians (`aggregate_step03_otu_medians_fast()`), whereas acropetal leaf dimorphism is modeled simultaneously through the orthogonal cauline and heteroblasty feature track (`Cauline_PC1..PC3`, `Cauline_ECT_PC1..PC2`, `basal_vs_cauline_solidity_delta`, `heteroblasty_ect_distance`).
  - **Level 2 (10 km Conspecific Spatial Deme Assignment):** In `assign_spatial_gathering_demes()`, conspecific individuals within a $10\text{ km}$ single-linkage Haversine radius (and shared gathering keys) receive deterministic `population_deme_id` and `deme_n_specimens` identifiers while preserving raw specimen morphometric coordinates for independent LOOCV and MANOVA hypothesis testing.
  - **Level 3 (Ontogenetic Phenophase Residualization, Photometry-Only Batch Centering and Hennig Ridgeline Merging):** In `scripts/morphometrics/plasticity_mitigation_utils.R`, `merge_voucher_phenophase_covariates()` joins optical capitulum states (`is_verified_anthesis`, `dominant_pappus_ratio`, and circular collection day `doy`), `residualize_ontogenetic_phenophase_plasticity()` removes within-taxon seasonal growth drift strictly on active pure-basal vouchers (`morph_tier_source == "Basal_Rosette"`), `apply_eb_institution_batch_centering()` logs `pre_eb_batch_partial_eta_sq` diagnostics across traits while restricting actual shrinkage subtraction to technical photometry columns (`^blade_(cie_|.*browning)`) when `allow_shape_centering = FALSE` and institutions are not regionally confounded (Cramer's $V \le 0.45$ and dominant regional share $< 0.75$), and `merge_plastic_gmm_components_ridgeline()` merges overlapping Gaussian mixture modes via Hennig ridgeline density connectivity (`ridgeline_ratio_thresh`) prior to downstream Step 07 lineage gating.
* **Two-Stage Gold/Silver Anchor Seeding, Dynamic Heteroblastic Sub-Modes and Confident Learning Pruning:**
  In `select_adaptive_reference_anchors()`, taxa with $\ge \max(5, p_{\text{eff}} + 3)$ `Tier_1_Gold` + `Tier_0_Type` anchors seed the initial FastMCD reference ellipsoid on Gold and Type specimens ($\chi^2_{0.95, p}$; minimum sample size $n \ge \max(12, 5p)$ per Rousseeuw & Van Driessen 1999) and screen candidate `Tier_2_Silver` vouchers against that envelope at $\chi^2_{0.90, p}$ before refitting the combined anchor covariance. When a taxon exhibits bimodal basal leaf dissection across its `Tier_1_Gold` anchors ($\ge 2$ dissected and $\ge 2$ entire Gold vouchers with basal solidity span $> 0.15$), sub-mode FastMCD screening evaluates whole and dissected morphotypes jointly without static species lists. Active anchors are screened via leave-one-out shrinkage discriminant posteriors $\hat{P}_{-i}(y = k \mid x_i)$ with per-class self-confidence thresholds $t_j$ (Northcutt et al. 2021). Anchors that violate their assigned class threshold while exceeding another class's threshold are flagged (`is_confident_label_noise = TRUE`), demoted to `"Passive_Confident_Noise_Demoted"`, and projected passively. Intact types receive retention immunity (`anchor_tier_source = "Tier_0_Type_Anchor"`), whereas extreme types ($> \chi^2_{0.99}$) are isolated from FastMCD scatter estimation. Taxon reference centers shrink toward the type medoid:
  $$\boldsymbol{\mu}_{\text{ref}, k} \leftarrow (1 - \gamma_k) \boldsymbol{\mu}_{\text{ref}, k} + \gamma_k \boldsymbol{\mu}_{\text{type}, k},\quad \gamma_k = \min\!\left(0.35,\, \frac{2.5\,n_{\text{eff, type}, k}}{N_{\text{gs}, k} + 2.5\,n_{\text{eff, type}, k}}\right)$$
  Under `min_anchors_per_taxon = 5L`, the adaptive promotion target scales proportionally with the taxon pool (`eff_min_anc = min(5, floor(0.5 * n_tx_pool))` when `n_gs >= 2` and `n_tx_pool >= 5`, `n_gs` when `n_gs >= 2` in tiny fixtures, `min(2, n_tx_pool)` when `n_gs == 1`, and `min(5, n_tx_pool)` when `n_gs == 0`), promoting central `Tier_3_Bronze` vouchers within $\chi^2_{0.80}$ (`is_promoted_bronze = TRUE`, `anchor_tier_source = "Promoted_Bronze"`).
* **Empirical Bayes Determiner Authority Weights (Institution-Invariant):**
  `compute_empirical_bayes_authority_weights()` evaluates leave-one-out PC concordance (`use_loo_pc`) unconditionally when $G \ge 2$ using determiner reliability $\theta^{\text{det}}_d$ ($\alpha_0 = 5.0$, prior reliabilities $\pi_{\text{PrimaryType}} = 0.999$, $\pi_{\text{SecType}} = 0.985$, $\pi_{\text{Gold}} = 0.95$, $\pi_{\text{Silver}} = 0.85$, $\pi_{\text{PromotedBronze}} = 0.65$, $\pi_{\text{Bronze}} = 0.35$; Dawid & Skene 1979; Gelman & Hill 2007) without herbarium `institutionCode` shrinkage so regional specimen holdings do not distort determiner weights. I update each specimen's authority weight via Raykar et al. (2010) multiplicative Bayesian posterior updating against its leave-one-out cross-modal likelihood $L_{\text{LOO}}(\mathbf{x}_i \mid y_i^{\text{obs}})$:
  $$w_i = \frac{\pi_{d(i)} \, L_{\text{LOO}}(\mathbf{x}_i \mid y_i^{\text{obs}})}{\pi_{d(i)} \, L_{\text{LOO}}(\mathbf{x}_i \mid y_i^{\text{obs}}) + (1 - \pi_{d(i)}) \, \bar{L}_{\text{LOO}}(\mathbf{x}_i \mid k \ne y_i^{\text{obs}})}$$
  Types remain exempt from downward shrinkage, and unpromoted Bronze vouchers receive $0.0$ in `active_passive` mode.

### 4.3 Klingenberg Allometry, Dual-Track CDA and MANOVA (`04_gmm_morphotools.R`)
* **Two-Sample Prediction-Variance Corrected Type Distance and Per-Species Canonical GMM Relabeling:**
  $$D^2_{\text{type}, i} = \left(1 + \frac{1}{n_{\text{eff, type}, k}}\right)^{-1} (\mathbf{x}_i - \boldsymbol{\mu}_{\text{type}, k})^\top \mathbf{S}_{\text{reg}}^{-1} (\mathbf{x}_i - \boldsymbol{\mu}_{\text{type}, k})$$
  persisted as `type_morph_mahal_d2`, alongside `nearest_type_morph_taxon`, basionym-level `nearest_type_basionym` and `nearest_type_basionym_d2` (preserving distinct centroids per `species_raw` basionym so homotypic *sensu stricto* types anchor the taxon baseline while heterotypic types identify available resurrection names), and per-species `is_typical_morphotype` (GMM cluster $c^*_k$). Because Gaussian Mixture Model component indices are arbitrary across runs, `.canonical_relabel_gmm_clusters()` relabels `mclust` mixture components so Cluster 1 ($c^* \equiv 1$) is always the component nearest the homotypic nomenclatural type medoid or highest-weighted `Tier_1_Gold` core and orders secondary components $2..G$ by Mahalanobis distance from Cluster 1, whereas `compute_type_specimen_morph_control()` evaluates `is_typical_morphotype` per species ($c^*_k$) across multi-species runs (Scrucca et al. 2016; Cadena et al. 2018). `BayesFactor_vs_Null` scales $2\Delta\text{BIC}$ by Kish's effective sample size ratio $N_{\text{eff}} / N_{\text{core}}$.
* **Klingenberg (2016) Allometric Slope Homogeneity & YAML Wiring (`step04_allometry_merger_utils.R`):**
  I load `morphometrics:` defaults (`correct_allometry`, `allometry_r2_threshold`, `num_pcs`, `max_k`, `random_seed`) via `load_step04_morphometrics_config()` and evaluate slope homogeneity across ruler-calibrated anchors (`is_step04_calibrated_mm()` masking `is_calibrated_mm == FALSE` plus `mp_estimated`, `predicted_from_megapixels`, `tick_lattice_unboxed`, and `uncalibrated` sources) through multivariate interaction models in `RRPP::lm.rrpp(..., weights = eff_weight)`:
  $$\mathbf{y}_{\text{shape}} = \beta_0 + \beta_1 \log_{10}(\text{CS}) + \boldsymbol{\gamma}_{\text{species}} + \boldsymbol{\delta} (\log_{10}(\text{CS}) \times \boldsymbol{\gamma}_{\text{species}}) + \boldsymbol{\epsilon}$$
  In `"auto"` mode, common-slope residual correction ($\mathbf{y}_{\text{corrected}} = \mathbf{y}_{\text{shape}} - \hat{\beta}_1 (\log_{10}(\text{CS}) - \overline{\log_{10}(\text{CS})})$) runs only when interaction slopes stay homogeneous ($p_{\text{interaction}} > 0.05$) and size accounts for meaningful variance ($R^2_{\text{size}} \ge R^2_{\text{threshold}}$ with $p_{\text{size}} < 0.05$); explicit `correct_mode = "true"` overrides the interaction gate when needed, whereas diverging interspecific slopes in `"auto"` retain raw symmetric harmonics untouched. During Step 04 trait merging and SQLite re-runs, `merge_manifest_petiole_traits_by_individual()` zeroes uncalibrated `petiole_length_mm` and `blade_length_mm` while keeping `petiole_to_blade_ratio` and Step 02 CIELAB photometry (`blade_cie_l_sheet_delta`, `blade_cie_a_browning_delta`, `blade_cie_b_yellowing_delta`), `merge_reproductive_traits_by_individual()` pairs `capitula_count` with `corymb_head_count_est` (`NA` for sterile `0`-head sheets on `log1p_capitulum_count`), and `.restore_raw_step03_from_leaf_harmonics_db()` restores raw `A1..D24`, `PC1..PCk`, `solidity`, `q25_solidity`, and CIELAB baselines directly from SQLite `leaf_efa_harmonics` so repeated Step 04 runs stay bit-identical without CSV artifacts.
* **Subspace Selection, Fold-Internal Deme-Blocked CDA, Ecoregion Quarantine & Batch ANCOVA:**
  CDA and GMM feature pools incorporate `aspect_ratio`, `petiole_to_blade_ratio`, `AR_inv`, and scale-free inflorescence branching `log1p_capitulum_count` (on reproductive plants with `capitula_count >= 1`) alongside LeafMachine3 eroded-lamina paper-normalized CIE $L^*$ reflectance and browning deltas (`blade_cie_l_norm`, `blade_cie_l_sheet_delta`, `blade_cie_a_browning_delta`, `blade_cie_b_yellowing_delta`; preservation-variance `blade_cie_l_sd_std` is held out from primary GMM and CDA covariance matrices and only `^blade_cie_` photometry receives empirical Bayes batch centering), `basal_vs_cauline_length_ratio`, `basal_vs_cauline_solidity_delta`, `heteroblasty_efa_distance`, `heteroblasty_ect_distance`, `cauline_solidity_med`, `cauline_dissection_index_med`, `cauline_ect_lobe_complexity`, `Cauline_PC1..PC3`, `ECT_PC1..PC3`, `Cauline_ECT_PC1..PC2`, `ect_lobe_complexity`, and `dissection_index` alongside `solidity` (pruned for collinearity at $|r| \le 0.75$). Transitionally zoned (`Transitional_Passive`) and cauline-only fallback (`Cauline_Only_Fallback`) individuals remain excluded from active anchor estimation and institutional batch audits (`audit_institutional_batch_effects()`, which runs marginal drop-1 `anova()` controlling for `species_standardized` plus non-aliased `regional_group`), so only verified `Basal_Rosette` individuals define reference covariance matrices. I apply Horn's Parallel Analysis capped by Williams & Titus (1988) $P_{\max} = \lfloor (N_{\min,\text{anchor}} - 1) / 3 \rfloor$. In `compute_weighted_shrinkage_cda_loocv()`, I evaluate strictly morphological, taxon-blind 10 km gathering/deme-blocked cross-validation (with zero `MASS::lda` `CV = TRUE` fallback) by running training-fold median imputation, 75%-core Mahalanobis training outlier trimming (type-exempt), `select_morphometric_subspace(..., allow_supervised_f)`, and Kish-$N_{\text{eff}}$ Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage CDA inside every fold without test-fold leakage. Track 1 evaluates Unsupervised GMM Morphotypes (`Morphotype_K1..Kn`) via Kass & Raftery (1995) $2\Delta\text{BIC}$ thresholds ($> 6$ strong, $> 10$ very strong) while suppressing post-selection supervised CV accuracy and parametric `gmm_cluster` MANOVA $p$-values (Cadena et al. 2018; Gao et al. 2024). Track 2 evaluates Supervised EPA Level III Ecoregion CDA biplots (`allow_supervised_f = FALSE` in deme CV) and *a priori* MANOVA (`species_standardized` and `regional_group`) with Benjamini-Hochberg FDR, while `.is_quarantined_ecoregion_coord()` masks `lat`/`lon` before `infer_epa_level3_ecoregion()` and restricts `is_centroid_or_inst_coord` sheets to passive projection rather than active ecoregion training anchors.
* **Entropy-Penalized `ICL` and Covariance-Locked Sequential `B = 199` Bootstrap LRT Gate (`gmm_icl_bootstrap_utils.R`):**
  Non-Gaussian skewness can inflate mixture `BIC` even inside a single biological lineage. In `run_gmm_cluster_analysis()` (respecting configured `max_k` across all sample sizes without an arbitrary cap at `N > 100`), I pass `bic_table` through `apply_gmm_icl_bootstrap_gate()`. `compute_gmm_icl_profile()` computes Integrated Complete-data Likelihood ($\text{ICL}_K = \text{BIC}_K - 2\,\text{Entropy}_K$) across $K = 1..K_{\max}$, while `run_gmm_pair_bootstrap_lrt()` locks bootstrap replicate covariance model families to the observed winning null and alternative `mclust` model names at all sample sizes and draws `B = 199` parametric bootstrap samples from the fitted $G = k - 1$ Gaussian mixture null to calibrate $2(\ell_k - \ell_{k-1})$. Metrics persist as `ICL`, `Delta_ICL_vs_Null`, `Boot_LRT_Stat_K1_vs_K2`, `Boot_LRT_P_K1_vs_K2`, `Boot_LRT_Stat_prev`, and `Boot_LRT_P_prev` inside `gmm_bayes_factors_summary.csv`. In intraspecific mode (`intraspecific_mode = TRUE`), any split $K \ge 2$ collapses to $K = 1$ unless both `Delta_ICL_vs_Null > 0` and `Boot_LRT_P_K1_vs_K2 < 0.05` hold simultaneously, whereas in interspecific mode $K \ge 2$ collapses to $K = 1$ whenever `Delta_ICL_vs_Null <= 0` and `Boot_LRT_P_K1_vs_K2 >= 0.05`; when $K_{\text{best}} > 2$ survives $K = 1$ vs. $K = 2$, sequential $G = k - 1$ vs. $G = k$ checks step down to the highest supported $k_{\text{sup}} \ge 2$ (`_SeqBootStepped`).
* **Open-Set Recognition (OSR) Class-Conditional Mahalanobis Novelty Scoring:**
  Closed-set discriminant posteriors force every specimen into a training taxon even when a sheet belongs to an unmodeled congener or novel lineage. To relax the closed-world assumption, `.compute_open_set_recognition_metrics()` computes class-conditional Mahalanobis distances in regularized morphospace across all candidate congeneric reference centroids in `data/plantmatics.sqlite` (Scheirer et al. 2013; Lee et al. 2018; Geng et al. 2020), and it exports the minimum congener distance `min_congener_mahal_d2` and closest congener `min_congener_taxon` alongside upper-tail chi-square novelty probability `open_set_novelty_p` and confident label-noise flag `is_confident_label_noise` to `otu_morphometrics` and `morphometric_flags.csv`.

## 5. Phase 5 Protocol: Morphometric-Independent Ecological Niche Modeling, Regime Discovery (`ecotype_regime`) and Land-Masked SDMs (`05_multimodal_spatial_rf.R`)

Historical collections frequently lack fine-scale GPS coordinates, as eighteenth and nineteenth-century collectors recorded general county or river valley descriptions rather than outcrop coordinates. At the same time, cross-modal taxonomy requires an ecological stream that never peeks at leaf shape or sequencing labels while discovering habitat differentiation. I keep Step 05 (`05_multimodal_spatial_rf.R`, `step05_config_geo_utils.R` `< 320` lines, `step05_phenology_ecotypes.R`, and `step05_sdm_maxent.R`) **100% independent of Step 04 morphometrics and Step 06 genomics**: `load_step05_macroecology_config()` reads `config/config.yaml` bounds (`cor_threshold = 0.70`, `vif_threshold = 5.0`, `cumulative_importance_cutoff = 0.85`, and `min_flowering_doy = 30`), whereas Step 05 never reads `otu_morphometrics`, `gmm_cluster`, or `genetic_lineage_assignments` before modeling (`--morphometrics` is ignored unless a caller explicitly passes a non-default standalone compatibility path, and even then only individual keys join), so ecological regime calls in Step 05 remain statistically clean inputs for Step 07 congruence tests.

Both classes of exclusions write directly to the 8-column `public_genbank_rogue_audit.csv`, which keeps contaminated accessions out of conspecific matching.
* **LORO Shrinkage DAPC, Masked Cross-Entropy `sNMF`, Hybrid `AICc` & Reticulation Testing (`06_genomic_phylogenomics.R`, `step06_model_selection.R`, `step06_reticulation_coancestry.R`):**
  In `06_genomic_phylogenomics.R` and `step06_model_selection.R`, I compute missingness-aware allele-sharing distances (`PCo1..PCo6`) paired with replicate concordance across `M`/`P` extraction pairs. Inside each Leave-One-Replicate-Out fold without test-fold leakage, `estimate_ledoit_wolf_lambda()` computes analytical Ledoit-Wolf (2004) covariance shrinkage clamped to `[0.01, 0.95]` before regularized DAPC scores `geno_posterior_prob`, while `genomic_model_metrics.csv` logs `selected_shrinkage_lambda` and `loro_cv_logloss` alongside `selected_k_cross_entropy`. Concurrently, `select_admixture_k_cross_entropy()` runs 5-fold masked cross-entropy across `K = 1..k_max` (including $K = 1$ panmixia), picks `best_k` via a 1-SE parsimony rule, and writes `admixture_k_model_selection.csv`, while `evaluate_ml_tree_model_and_support()` audits ML/ME substitution models and clade bootstrap support against the public ASTRAL backbone (`Robinson-Foulds` distance) into `genomic_clade_cohesion_summary.csv`. For admixed candidates, `compute_hybrid_index_q12()` fits a 7-model multinomial likelihood surface across $(h, Q_{12})$ over ancestry-informative SNPs ($\Delta p \ge 0.25$); `AICc` weights (`best_model_aicc`, `akaike_weight`, `delta_aicc_vs_pure`) paired with Benjamini-Hochberg likelihood-ratio tests (`lrt_q_bh`) classify specimens across `Pure_Parent_1`, `Pure_Parent_2`, `F1_Hybrid`, `F2_Hybrid`, `BC1_Parent_1`, `BC1_Parent_2`, `Advanced_Introgressant`, and `Divergent_NonHybrid_Lineage`. On shared-haplotype clusters and quartets, `step06_reticulation_coancestry.R` fits `fineRADstructure` coancestry communities alongside tree-guided Patterson's $D$ / $f_b$ branch introgression (`Dsuite` auto-detected or built-in $D$-statistic solver), while `compute_quartet_concordance_snps2cf()` averages concordance across 16 quartets under an $M_0$ vs. $M_1$ $G$-test that zeros `snaq_gamma_minor` unless `delta_aicc_reticulation >= 2.0` and `g_test_p < 0.05`.
* **Invariant-Site Substitution Selection & Coalescent Delimitation (`step06_coalescent_models.py`, `step06_coalescent_delimitation.py`):**
  Because polymorphic-only SNP matrices bias branch-length ratios under small sample sizes ($n = 1$ to $2$), I parse full concatenated `PHYLIP`/`NEXUS` alignments that retain invariant sites and IUPAC heterozygote codes across $122\text{ bp}$ locus blocks. Inside `step06_coalescent_models.py`, `select_nucleotide_substitution_model()` evaluates five substitution models (`JC69`, `K80`, `F81`, `HKY85`, `TN93`) with and without `+G4`, plus Lewis (2001) `+ASC` conditional likelihood when invariant sites fall below $5\%$ (`prop_invariant < 0.05` and `n_sites >= 30`). Candidates rank by `BIC` and `AICc` inside `substitution_model_selection.csv`, and model diagnostics write to `coalescent_model_adequacy.csv`. Using the winning `ti_tv_kappa` and `gamma_alpha` in `compute_model_corrected_divergence_blocks()`, I compute within-lineage $\hat{\theta}_A$, model-corrected $d_{XY}$, net divergence $\tau_{\text{net}} = \max(0, \frac{1}{2}(d_{XY} - \bar{\theta}))$, and $B = 250$ to $500$ locus-bootstrapped Genealogical Divergence Index estimates (`coalescent_gdi`, Leaché et al. 2019; Jackson et al. 2017):
  $$\widehat{gdi} = 1 - \exp\!\left(-\frac{2\,\hat{\tau}_{\text{net}}}{\max(10^{-4},\, \hat{\theta}_A)}\right)$$
  Values $\widehat{gdi} < 0.20$ diagnose a single oversplit lineage, whereas $\widehat{gdi} > 0.70$ with $Q_{12} < 0.15$ supports distinct species-level coalescence. To test secondary contact vs. pure ILS directly across blocks, `compute_coalescent_introgression_bf()` compares a single-rate coalescent null ($M_0$) against a two-component introgression mixture ($M_1$) via `bpp_stepping_stone` or `bic_coalescent_mixture` (`bf_10`, `w_introgr`, `tau_introgr`, `tau_sp`). In parallel, `step06_coalescent_delimitation.py` runs 200-replicate quartet block-bootstraps (`quartet_boot_support` and `quartet_p_value`) in `svdquartets_support.csv`. That exporter also sets `model = HKY` or `JC69` dynamically inside `bpp_msci.ctl` and writes `substitution_model` alongside `bf_method` into `coalescent_gdi_delimitation.csv`.
* **Small-Sample Guardrails and Post-Hoc Landscape Genomics (`step06_spatial_landscape_genomics.R`, `step06_phylogenomics_utils.R`):**
  When median sample count per population falls below $5$, Step 06 sets `small_n_guardrail_active = TRUE` and explicitly disables both Weir & Cockerham $F_{ST}$ outlier scans (`OutFLANK`, `BayeScan`) and SFS or geographic-cline methods (`dadi`, `fastsimcoal2`, Tajima's $D$, `HZAR`). Once Step 06 has written `genetic_lineage_assignments` independently to SQLite, `step06_spatial_landscape_genomics.R` and `.compute_posthoc_congruence()` filter out `is_centroid_or_inst_coord` vouchers alongside unvouchered herbarium rooftops (`<= 0.25 km`), `(0, 0)`, and US/CA positive-longitude flips via `.filter_step06_valid_geo_idx()` before querying read-only Step 04/05 covariates strictly post-hoc to compare nested Wang (2013) Multiple Matrix Regression with Randomization (`MMRR`) models (`IBD_only`, `IBE_only`, `IBD_plus_IBE`, full `MMRR` over 9,999 matrix permutations into `mmrr_model_selection.csv`). Alongside `conStruct`-style spatial vs. non-spatial decay, `run_step06_landscape_genomics()` selects `feems_cv_lambda` via leave-one-node-out cross-validation (`landscape_mmrr_feems_summary.csv`) and exports `fig06a_genomic_pcoa_tree_concordance.png` and `fig06b_reticulation_gdi_landscape.png` alongside the 4-panel `fig06c_cytonuclear_paralog_cytotype_audit.png` (`scripts/figures/step06c_figures.R`; Panel A `HDplot` paralog and `Compositae-ParaLoss-1272` ortholog screen, Panel B skimmed plastome/nrITS/`waxy` cytonuclear concordance, Panel C public backbone `PhyTop` ILS vs. introgression conflict, and Panel D `nQuire` cytotype `BIC` distributions paired with `CCDB`/`ICNA` SQLite priors).

## 7. Phase 7 Protocol: Reliability-Weighted Consensus and Hierarchical Nomenclatural Type Decision Matrix (`07_triage_dashboard_synthesis.R`)

In Phase 7, I synthesize all independent lines of evidence. Suppose a specimen displays an anomalous leaf shape yet matches its type locality across rhizosphere soils and flowering phenology alongside unlinked nextRAD ancestry; a single-modality model would misclassify that plant as a distinct species. By combining independent data streams, I resolve taxonomic status while giving no single noisy stream unchecked veto power.

* **Master View Synthesis and Modality Reliability Weighting (`compute_modality_reliability_weights`, `compute_probabilistic_consensus_scores`):**
  I synthesize consensus by querying `v_integrative_triage_master`, which joins independent Step 04 morphometrics, Step 05 `ecotype_regime` regimes and rhizosphere *z*-scores (`edaphic_z_score`, `type_edaphic_z_score`), circular Hopkins phenology (`hopkins_adjusted_doy`, `delta_doy_resid`), and Step 06 `genetic_lineage_assignments` (`geno_predicted_taxon`, `hybrid_generation_class`, `interspecific_ancestry_q12`, `coalescent_gdi`, `public_plastome_match`, `public_its_match`, `waxy_lineage_match`, `public_backbone_clade`, `public_phytop_ils_index`, `public_phytop_ih_index`, plus companion `public_backbone_concordance.csv` and `public_organellar_placement.csv` summaries) onto `plant_individuals`. In multi-species cohorts ($N_{\text{taxa}} \ge 2$), I weight each stream by empirical out-of-fold Cohen's $\kappa_m$ (`cda_predicted_taxon` on active `Basal_Rosette` individuals, `edaphic_best_fit_taxon` or `rf_predicted_taxon`, leave-one-out circular Hopkins flowering discrimination, and `G1`/`G2` genomic LORO accuracy `w_geno_kappa`) modulated by class-conditional precision $C^{(m)}_{k,j}$:
  $$w_{i,m} = w_m \cdot (0.5 + 0.5 \cdot \text{Precision}_m(\hat{y}_{i,m}))$$
  Unsequenced vouchers retain exact `w_geno = 0` weight renormalization when genomic sample payloads are absent or during consensus weighting so zero-genomics runs remain invariant, whereas whenever sequenced `G1` or `G2` accessions sit in the cohort I route unsequenced vouchers strictly through the four-tier genomic propagation and abstention guardrails in `step07_genomic_propagation.R`. In single-taxon and intraspecific cohorts ($N_{\text{taxa}} = 1$), `compute_modality_reliability_weights()` computes `w_morph_kappa` directly from the active `Basal_Rosette` inlier rate (`min(0.80, max(0.30, 0.65 * inlier_rate))`), derives `w_edaphic_kappa` from OOF Cohen's $\kappa$ between independent Step 05 `ecotype_regime` labels and `rf_predicted_taxon` whenever $K_{\text{eco}} \ge 2$ (falling back to centered `spatial_block_cv_acc` when unimodal), and evaluates reproductive isolation continuously via Hopkins phenology residuals (`min(1.0, abs(delta_doy_resid) / 30.0)`).
* **Taxon-Agnostic 5-Stage Hierarchical Decision Matrix:**
  - **Sibling Ramet Morphology Propagation and Same-Sheet Gathering Coherence (`catalogNumber`):** On multi-plant herbarium sheets, leafless or flowering-only ramets (`plant_individual_id`) inherit measured foliar morphology from conspecific sibling ramets on the same `catalogNumber` sheet before decision scoring, and I enforce same-sheet (`catalogNumber`) gathering coherence across sibling `plant_individual_id` ramets while preserving immutable `'Confirmed_Type_Control'` and `'Type_Synonymy_Candidate'` calls without overwriting type outcomes.
  - **Rule A (Nomenclatural Type Override):** I never reassign type specimens (`recommended_determination = given_sp`), nor do I route them to `Misidentification_Reassign`, `Hybrid_Introgressant`, or `Putative_Cryptic_Species`. Concordant types receive `taxonomic_status_call = "Confirmed_Type_Control"` (`RESOLVED`). Cross-modal divergence triggers `taxonomic_status_call = "Type_Synonymy_Candidate"` (`CRITICAL`). Both calls remain protected verbatim through sibling coherence and table finalization.
  - **Rule B and Stage 1 Precedence (`Misidentification_Reassign`, Self-Ellipsoid Exclusion, and Authority-Scaled Multi-Stream Congruence):** `apply_taxonomic_decision_matrix()` evaluates Stage 1 misidentification and Open-Set congener reassignment (`congener_osr_reassign`) strictly before Stage 2 hybrid and Stage 3 cryptic lineage routing, using 10 km deme-blocked out-of-fold CDA posteriors (`cda_oof_posterior_prob`), per-class Confident Learning self-confidence thresholds (`cl_target_threshold`; Northcutt et al. 2021), shared-USDA-symbol 2-letter epithet prefix disambiguation across taxonomic synonym transfers, and exact `self_morph_df` within-taxon Mahalanobis tolerance ellipsoids (`self_morph_mahal_d2` against $\chi^2_{0.95, df}$; Zapata & Jiménez 2012). Any voucher inside its recorded species' 95% self-ellipsoid is protected from reassignment across overlapping congeners. On single-plant sheets outside the 95% self-ellipsoid, `Tier_1_Gold` specialist annotations require corroboration from at least one independent non-outline stream (rhizosphere/climatic fit, calibrated `blade_cie_l_norm` vestiture or involucre geometry, flowering phenology, or exsiccatae duplicates), and `Tier_2_Silver` annotations are protected whenever independent ecological, phenological, or vestiture reflectance evidence supports `given_sp` (Goodwin et al. 2015; Padial et al. 2010). For `congener_osr_reassign`, multi-species runs test exact `open_set_df` chi-square bounds ($D^2_{\text{Mahal}}(k') \le \chi^2_{0.90, df_{\text{OSR}}}$), whereas `--intraspecific-mode` enforces a stricter ceiling ($\chi^2_{0.50, df_{\text{OSR}}}$ paired with a `<= 250 km` regional sympatry guard against the nearest congeneric voucher centroid) across run-invariant Fourier harmonics, `blade_cie_l_norm` reflectance, and topological ECT predictors.
  - **Rule C (*Sensu Stricto* Typical Morphotype Locking):** Specimens in their species' canonically relabeled typical morphotype GMM cluster ($c^*_k$) with $z_{\text{type}} \le 2.0$ are locked as `Confirmed_Species` (*sensu stricto*).
  - **Stage 2 (`Hybrid_Introgressant` via 5-Gate Population Contact-Zone Swarm Protocol):** I disable interspecific hybrid evaluation completely in `--intraspecific-mode` and whenever fewer than 2 standardized *Packera* species are present in the cohort. In multi-species runs, I require five joint gates before elevating any non-type basal-rosette voucher to `Hybrid_Introgressant`: (1) verified physical syntopy of both parent species within `15 km` (or `25 km` within the same county), (2) overlapping Hopkins-adjusted flowering windows (`21 days` or less peak separation) and shared microhabitat tolerance (`edaphic_z <= 2.5`), (3) pairwise parental Mahalanobis chord projection $t_{AB} \in [0.22, 0.78]$ with orthogonal residual $d^2_{\perp} \le \chi^2_{0.90, p-1}$, (4) multi-organ corroboration across leaf outline and vestiture reflectance, involucre geometry, or foliar fluctuating asymmetry alongside non-certain posterior split, and (5) population-level contact-zone replication (`hybrid_zone_cohesion_pass == TRUE`, requiring at least 2 independent admixed vouchers in the local contact zone for the same parent pair unless explicitly annotated with a hybrid cross on the herbarium label). Confirmed hybrid swarm vouchers export explicit putative parent binomials in `hybrid_parent_1` and `hybrid_parent_2` alongside `recommended_determination` (`Parent 1 x Parent 2`).
  - **Quality-Gated Organellar Polarity & Non-Mutating Out-of-Panel Rationale (`step07_organellar_triage_utils.R`):** Using `hydrate_step07_organellar_placement_metrics()` paired with `evaluate_step07_organellar_and_oop_signals()`, I load per-sample 11-mer containment and shared k-mer counts from `public_organellar_placement.csv` across voucher and `G1` isoduplicate rows while keeping un-scored fixtures unaffected. Stage 1 (`Misidentification_Reassign`) never reads `public_plastome_match`, `public_its_match`, or `waxy_lineage_match`. On pure nuclear `G1`/`G2` rows (`geno_posterior_prob >= 0.80` with `Q12 < 0.15` or non-admixed `NA`), a quality-gated plastome match (`containment >= 0.80` and `11mers >= 80`) to a distinct canonical *Packera* species without matching quality-gated `ITS` or `waxy` folds into `geno_cytonuc_dv` so `geno_ancient_introg_v` blocks false Stage 2 hybrid elevation and records `cpDNA=...` provenance. On proven admixed rows (`Q12 >= 0.35` or `is_geno_admixed`), `hq_donor_hint` (`containment >= 0.80` and `11mers >= 80`) serves strictly as a fallback when `geno_parent_2` is missing, and confirmed Stage 2 hybrids receive `[Maternal cpDNA Parent: ...; Putative Pollen Donor: ...]` polarity notes when the quality-gated plastome matches one confirmed parent. Finally, sequenced specimens where both `ITS` and `waxy` (`containment >= 0.85` and `11mers >= 80`) match one canonical *Packera* taxon absent from sequenced NextRAD reference panel anchors receive a non-mutating `[Out-of-Panel Nuclear Locus Match: ITS+waxy=...]` rationale without changing any status call or determination.
  - **Stage 3 (`Putative_Cryptic_Species`, Spatial-Cline Rejection, Coalescent Oversplit Block, and Heterotypic Basionym Resurrection):** To prevent duplicate exsiccatae sheets, core-cluster late-season taphonomic outliers ($g_{\text{cl}} = 1$), or continuous geographic clines from inflating cryptic lineage counts, I require `g_cl > 1`, `!is_taphonomic_plasticity`, `cryptic_cluster_cohesion_pass == TRUE`, and `cryptic_non_clinal_pass == TRUE` (Carstens et al. 2013; Sukumaran & Knowles 2017; Fišer et al. 2018; Chambers & Hillis 2020), while applying a Phase 2 Coalescent Oversplit Block whenever sequenced or cluster-propagated members (`coalescent_gdi` or `cluster_propagated_gdi`) show $\widehat{gdi} < 0.20$ (`Single_Lineage_Oversplit` routes to `Ecophenotypic_Plasticity` or `Confirmed_Species`, whereas $\widehat{gdi} > 0.70$ paired with $Q_{12} < 0.15$ or `Divergent_NonHybrid_Lineage` independently corroborates `Putative_Cryptic_Species` when `!tier0_abstain_dv`):
    1. $\ge 5$ exsiccatae-deduplicated gathering events (`exsiccatae_key` and surname-normalized `recordedBy + recordNumber`) distributed across $\ge 3$ independent 10 km spatial demes (`population_deme_id`) and $\ge 2$ collectors or herbaria;
    2. Cluster-median `min_congener_mahal_d2` $> \chi^2_{0.95, df_{\text{OSR}}}$ (`open_set_novelty_p < 0.05` against all known congeners in exact `open_set_df` space);
    3. $\ge 50\%$ of cluster members share concordant quantitative ecological ($D_{\text{edaph}} < 0.30$ or $z_{\text{edaph}} > 2.5$) or phenological ($|\Delta\text{DOY}_{\text{resid}}| \ge \tau_{\text{cryptic}}$) barriers with $\text{BF} \ge 10.0$;
    4. Allopatric morphotype clusters explained by a smooth linear geographic cline ($R^2 \ge 0.60$ against latitude and longitude without parapatric contact within 50 km, Hopkins flowering isolation, or azonal lithology shift) set `cryptic_non_clinal_pass = FALSE` and route to `Subspecies_Ecotype`.
    Cohesive non-clinal lineages matching a heterotypic nomenclatural basionym type within $\chi^2_{0.95, p}$ export `resurrection_basionym` and `taxonomic_status_call = "Taxonomic_Resurrection_Candidate"` (`Evaluate_Basionym_Resurrection`), reserving unnamed `Putative Cryptic Lineage K<c>` strictly for lineages outside all known congener and basionym type ellipsoids. Before final persistence, `evaluate_step07_hydro_edaphic_and_basionym_guards()` (`scripts/analysis/edaphic_geochem_wetland_utils.R`) checks each candidate against SQLite `taxon_backbone_profiles` homotypic versus heterotypic synonym arrays (`basionym_synonymy_type`), clearing `resurrection_basionym` to `NA_character_` whenever `basionym_synonymy_type == "Homotypic"` so nomenclatural combinations never masquerade as distinct heterotypic species, while preserving verified `Heterotypic` basionyms intact.
  - **Stage 4 (`Subspecies_Ecotype` vs. `Ecophenotypic_Plasticity`):** Evaluates cohesive geographic or edaphic divergence for ecotypes versus overlapping envelopes for plasticity alongside Step 07 hydro-edaphic habitat classification (`hydro_edaphic_niche_regime`) and USFWS NWI / USACE NWPL moisture fidelity concordance (`wetland_fidelity_concordance`).
* **Non-Circular Cytological Linkage Plus Postzygotic Heteroploid Barriers and Gigas Audit (`step07_cytology_integration.R`):**

### Four-Tier Unsequenced Voucher Genomic Propagation and Abstention Protocol (`step07_genomic_propagation.R`)

Herbarium collections exhibit high misidentification rates across institutions (Goodwin et al. 2015), whereas reduced-representation sequencing covers only a fraction of mounted sheets. When sequenced `G1` or `G2` accessions are absent from the cohort, Step 07 leaves baseline consensus scores alongside authority weights and treatments strictly unchanged (`genomic_propagation_tier = NA`). When sequenced vouchers are present, I bridge genomic certainty onto unsequenced specimens strictly through four gated tiers in `step07_genomic_propagation.R`, so sympatric contact zones avoid blind label copying.

* **Tier 1 (`G1_Exsiccatae_Isoduplicate`) and Mixed-Gathering Mahalanobis Veto:**
  When a `G1_Direct_Voucher` sample sequences one duplicate of an exsiccatae gathering sharing `exsiccatae_key`, Step 06 (`_link_sample_to_voucher()`) resolves duplicate key hits toward the primary imaged sheet (`is_primary_duplicate == TRUE`), and I propagate that genomic identity across unsequenced duplicates under `G1_Exsiccatae_Isoduplicate` with a `0.95` link weight scale (Nicolson et al. 2018). Because historical collectors occasionally mounted heterogeneous taxa under one gathering number, blind duplicate propagation risks spreading mixed-mount errors across herbaria (Goodwin et al. 2015). I therefore evaluate `Basal_Rosette` Mahalanobis distance against the sequenced duplicate (`deme_mahal_d2`), and I immediately veto propagation whenever `deme_mahal_d2 > qchisq(0.99, p)`, when an unsequenced sheet carries `is_type_specimen == TRUE`, or when a multi-plant sheet carries a morphometric outlier flag (`is_multi && is_mo`).
* **Tier 2 (`G2_Conditional_Deme_Hull`) Conflict-Only Gates, Soft Attenuation, and Micro-Sympatry Guard:**
  Neighboring rosettes from a single homogeneous outcrop often belong to one breeding deme, so I propagate local deme identity (`G2_Conditional_Deme_Hull`, weighted at baseline `0.75` and scaled softly across `[0.45, 0.75]` via `geno_deme_weight_scale` without unilateral Stage 1 misidentification override) whenever an unsequenced `Basal_Rosette` voucher clears four conflict-only gates. First, the specimen must lie within `<= 2.0 km` of a QC-passing sequenced `G1`/`G2` or georeferenced `G3` anchor with `coordinateUncertaintyInMeters <= 2000 m` or missing precision (`NA`), and it receives a `0.85x` soft weight factor whenever precision lies in `(1000, 2000] m` or an `NA`-precision voucher sits at `(1.0, 2.0] km`. Second, `is_azonal_lithology` must match the anchor when both values are finite, and soil chemistry passes cleanly at `type_edaphic_z_score <= 1.75` or local anchor `fine_soil_ph` agreement (`<= 0.60`), takes a `0.88x` attenuation factor across `(1.75, 2.50]` or up to `3.0` with local pH difference `<= 1.00`, and vetoes beyond those bounds. Third, the voucher must lie inside the 95% `Basal_Rosette` Mahalanobis hull (`deme_mahal_d2 <= qchisq(0.95, p)`) while flowering within `tau_deme_doy` (clipped to `[24, 28]` days, default `26.0`) of the local anchor's active-anthesis `hopkins_adjusted_doy` or taxon mean, with `0.88x` attenuation across `(18, tau_deme_doy]` days. Fourth, my micro-sympatry guard modeled on mixed sites such as Buck Creek and `VAL1` evaluates only verified anchors (`is_type_specimen` and `Tier_0` DNA anchors alongside non-outlier `Tier_1_Gold` vouchers) within `<= 5.0 km` for genomic anchors or `<= 2.0 km` for cohort anchors and aborts when $D^2_{\text{alt}} - \text{deme\_mahal\_d2} < 4.0$, so unsequenced `Tier_2_Silver` or `Tier_3_Bronze` misidentifications never poison pure sites. Across multi-cytotype taxa, `G2` transfer vetoes strictly on observed cytotype conflicts inside the local `2.0 km` deme rather than missing ploidy or distant allopatric populations, while applying a `0.85x` soft discount whenever recipient or local deme ploidy is unobserved.
* **Tier 3 (`Tier_0` Weight `4.0` Anchor Calibration, Contradicted Anchor Eviction, and Four-Condition Abstention):**
  DNA-verified accessions establish rigorous calibration standards for morphological discrimination when historical determinations disagree (Marhold et al. 2010; Giaco et al. 2022; Rocha et al. 2023). I assign verified `G1` sequenced anchors `Tier_0` weight (`4.0`) for reference centroids, circular phenology, and discriminant training while protecting species centroids against poisoned metadata: whenever a `Tier_1_Gold` or lower voucher is contradicted by its own `G1` sequence at posterior probability `>= 0.85` (or displays admixed `interclass_het_Q12 >= 0.35`), `resolve_tier0_anchor_overrides()` sets `evict_from_given_anchor = TRUE` so Step 07 (`07_triage_dashboard_synthesis.R`) removes that specimen from reference morphology and phenology pools for its nominal `given_sp`. Evaluating leave-one-population-out cross-validation (`LOPO-CV`) on a temperature-scaled Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage discriminant classifier (`lambda = 0.20`) anchored on vetted `Tier_0` specimens, I populate `tier0_calibrated_taxon` and `tier0_calibrated_prob` (`tier0_abstention_flag = 0`) on well-separated unsequenced specimens and inject a soft consensus prior (`w_t0 = 0.50 * lopo_cv_kappa`) only when `geno_link_scale == 0.0`, `tier0_abstention_flag == 0`, and `tier0_calibrated_prob >= 0.80`. Conversely, abstention triggers set `tier0_abstention_flag = 1`, record `tier0_calibrated_taxon = "Sensu_Lato_DNA_Required"`, block Stage 3 and Stage 4 splitting calls, and append `[Tier_0 Abstention: Phenotypic overlap across genetically distinct lineages; DNA required]` to `synthesis_rationale` whenever `p1 < 0.70`, top-two posterior margin `p1 - p2 < 0.15`, `LOPO-CV` pairwise error `> 0.20` with `p1 < 0.90`, or minimum Mahalanobis distance exceeds `qchisq(0.975, d)` (Carstens et al. 2013).
* **Tier 4 Cluster-Wide `gdi` Governance (`cluster_propagated_gdi`):**
  Coalescent species delimitation via the genealogical divergence index ($\widehat{gdi} = 1 - e^{-2\tau/\theta}$) separates shallow population structure ($\widehat{gdi} < 0.20$) from isolated evolutionary lineages ($\widehat{gdi} > 0.70$; Jackson et al. 2017; Carstens et al. 2013). Rather than letting missing ploidy counts or distant allopatric polyploids block unsequenced local members, `propagate_cluster_gdi_governance()` aggregates `G1` and `G2` `coalescent_gdi` estimates per taxon and `coancestry_cluster` alongside regional (`<= 250 km`) `gmm_cluster` lineages while suppressing transfer strictly when observed cytotypes conflict inside the exact `coancestry_cluster` or local `2.0 km` deme alongside conflicts against the recipient's known ploidy. Admixed rows and evicted misidentified anchors alongside `tier0_abstention_flag == 1` vouchers stay excluded. Whenever `cluster_propagated_gdi < 0.20`, Stage 3 `Putative_Cryptic_Species` is blocked across unsequenced members (`[Cluster Coalescent Oversplit Block: propagated gdi=...]`). Conversely, when `cluster_propagated_gdi > 0.70` and `tier0_abstention_flag != 1` inside a validated lineage hull, unsequenced cluster members receive `[Candidate Member via Cluster-Propagated gdi=...]` corroboration alongside standard gathering and non-clinal gates.

### Quantitative Taxon-Complex Delimitation Within Phase 7 (`taxon_complex_discovery_utils.R`)

Within Phase 7, I also scale beyond single-species comparisons to delimit species complexes across the genus. At first glance, local ecotypes and distinct species complexes seem to require different analytical approaches, but that difference is superficial because both represent continuous divergence surfaces across morphospace and ecological landscapes.

* **Multimodal Pairwise Affinity Calculation:**
  I calculate pairwise morphological and fine-scale edaphic distances alongside climatic and phenological distances across all active taxa, and I shrink taxon centroids toward nomenclatural type baselines with weight $\gamma_k$.
* **Unsupervised Graph Modularity and Hierarchical Delimitation:**
  I discover species complexes through unsupervised graph modularity (Leiden and Louvain algorithms) and Ward.D2 hierarchical clustering, which resolves Taxon by GMM Morphotype subclusters when $N_{\text{taxa}} \le 2$.
* **Complex Discovery Plates and Neighbor Exports (`taxon_complex_discovery_utils.R`):**
  I export affinity networks and hierarchical dendrograms (`fig07b_taxon_complex_delimitation_network.png`) alongside pairwise distance summaries and neighbor lists (`empirical_taxon_complexes.csv`, `pairwise_taxon_affinity_matrix.csv`, `focal_auto_neighbors.txt`), which provides quantitative evidence for species complex boundaries.
